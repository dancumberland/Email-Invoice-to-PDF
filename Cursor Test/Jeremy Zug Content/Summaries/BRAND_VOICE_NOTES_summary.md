# Brand Voice Analysis Summary

## Summary
The brand voice is positioned as an approachable expert in mental health billing and credentialing, balancing professionalism with accessibility. The voice is designed to be empathetic to providers' needs while maintaining authority in the field.

## Voice Markers
- **Approachable**: "We take high level billing concepts and make them easily relatable"
- **Smart**: "We know what we're talking about, and we're quick witted"
- **Empathetic**: "We feel your pain"
- **Fun**: "We use humor to engage with our customers and connect"
- **Empowering**: "We believe in our providers capabilities and their choices"

## Notable Quotes
1. "Our brand voice is empathetic to the provider's feelings. We are an approachable friend empowering providers to make the best decisions for their practice."
2. "We know how to have fun while demonstrating expert smarts."
3. "We want providers to feel: calm, trust, understanding, confidence, peace, conviction, directionality"

## Voice Positioning
- Elite vs. Mass Appeal: Slightly leaning toward Mass Appeal
- Serious vs. Playful: Gently tilting toward Playful
- Conventional vs. Rebel: Balanced at midpoint
- Friend vs. Authority: Slightly leaning toward Friend
- Mature & Classic vs. Young & Innovative: Closer to Young & Innovative

## Content Guidelines
1. **Word Length**: Prefer shorter words for clarity and directness
2. **Sentence Length**: Average 14 words, avoid run-ons
3. **Tempo**: Pain point → Fact → Commentary → Possibilities
4. **Pronouns**: Use "we" and "Practice Solutions" for company, "providers/you" for audience
5. **Jargon**: Define technical terms in relatable language
6. **Contractions**: Allowed and encouraged
7. **Emotional Tone**: Focus on positive emotions (calm, trust, confidence) while acknowledging provider challenges
