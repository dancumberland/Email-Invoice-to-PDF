# Blog Platform Guide

## Platform Overview
Our blog serves as a comprehensive resource for mental health providers, offering in-depth guidance on insurance billing, credentialing, and practice management. The content balances technical accuracy with accessibility, making complex topics approachable for our audience.

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Expert Yet Accessible
1. **Balance**
   - Maintain professional authority
   - Explain complex concepts clearly
   - Use relatable examples
   - Keep tone supportive and encouraging

2. **Writing Style**
   - Clear, direct language
   - Active voice
   - Concise paragraphs
   - Logical flow

### Content Structure
1. **Introduction**
   - Clear title with value proposition
   - Brief overview of topic
   - Relevance to audience
   - Preview of key points

2. **Main Content**
   - Clear section headings
   - Progressive information flow
   - Practical examples
   - Supporting data and tables

3. **Conclusion**
   - Summary of key points
   - Next steps or actions
   - Call to action
   - Supportive closing

## Best Practices

### Do's
1. **Content Creation**
   - Use clear, descriptive titles
   - Include relevant images
   - Link to related content
   - Provide practical examples
   - Include actionable steps

2. **Technical Elements**
   - Use proper headings (H1, H2, H3)
   - Include meta descriptions
   - Optimize for SEO
   - Ensure mobile responsiveness

3. **Formatting**
   - Short paragraphs (3-5 sentences)
   - Bullet points for lists
   - Tables for complex data
   - Consistent styling

### Don'ts
1. **Content**
   - Avoid overly technical language
   - Don't use multiple analogies
   - Avoid political statements
   - Don't use swearing or inappropriate humor
   - Avoid run-on sentences

2. **Structure**
   - Don't skip headings
   - Avoid wall of text
   - Don't overuse bold/italic
   - Avoid inconsistent formatting
