# 🔄 6 Domains of Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)

This graphic illustrates the essential stages of a complete billing and administrative cycle. These domains are the foundation of efficient revenue collection and claim management in healthcare practices.

---

## ➤ Credentialing  
The process of enrolling your providers with insurance panels to allow billing under your organization's tax ID. This is the first gate to ensure your services are reimbursable.

---

## ➤ Patient Registration  
Accurate intake of patient demographics, insurance data, and consent forms. This step ensures claims are submitted with valid, matching information.

---

## 🔄 Core Billing Cycle

### 🔁 Eligibility & Benefits (E&B)  
Verify each patient's coverage before service is rendered to avoid denials and billing surprises. Includes co-pay, deductible, and plan limitations.

### 📤 Claim Submission  
Prepare and submit claims based on documentation. This step requires precise CPT/ICD-10 coding and payer-specific formatting rules.

### 📈 Aging Follow-Up  
Unpaid claims are tracked and followed up regularly. Includes resubmissions, appeals, and communications with payers.

### 💵 Payment Posting  
Record payments and adjustments in the EHR. This step provides the basis for accurate patient balances and financial reporting.
