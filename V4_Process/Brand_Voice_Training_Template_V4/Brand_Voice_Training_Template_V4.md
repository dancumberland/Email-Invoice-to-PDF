## START HERE: Generating Your Comprehensive Brand Voice System

**Objective:** This template guides the AI in creating a single, highly detailed **Master Brand Voice Document** (target 30-50 pages) by systematically processing all provided client context materials. Subsequently, it will help generate in-depth **Platform-Specific Add-On Guides**.

**Process Overview:**

1.  **Provide Context Materials:** Gather all relevant documents for the target thought leader (e.g., website copy, blog posts, podcast transcripts, social media profiles, ICP documents).
2.  **Initiate Master Document Generation:** Use the AI instructions below. The AI will first log all documents and then process them sequentially, extracting insights and populating the 17 sections of the Master Brand Voice Document.
3.  **Review and Refine Master Document:** Once the AI completes the Master Document, review it for accuracy, completeness, and depth.
4.  **Initiate Platform-Specific Guide Generation:** For each desired platform, use the AI instructions to generate an Add-On Guide. This guide will adapt the core brand voice for platform-specific nuances.
5.  **Review and Refine Platform Guides:** Review each platform guide for relevance and actionable detail.

**Key to Success:** The AI is instructed to be extremely detailed, providing multiple examples, counter-examples, and practical implementation advice for every subsection. Ensure your source materials are comprehensive to enable this depth.
