# 🩺 Practice Health Check  
**A comprehensive look at your practice's billing and administrative processes**

---

## 🧠 Overview

**Practice Solutions** is a medical billing company that supports **mental and behavioral healthcare providers** in achieving optimal revenue cycle management.

The **Practice Health Check** is an in-depth review of your practice’s operations across **seven key administrative and billing domains**. Over two full business days, a **Practice Wellness Expert** collaborates with your team to:

- Benchmark your systems against best practices
- Identify strengths and weaknesses
- Provide tailored recommendations for improvement

At the end of the process, you'll receive a full summary, highlighting:

- ✅ What you’re doing well  
- ⚠️ What needs improvement  
- 💡 How to fix it

---

## 🔍 Domains Evaluated

1. **Credentialing**  
2. **Payment Posting**  
3. **Human Resources**  
4. **Eligibility & Benefits**  
5. **Aging Follow-Up**  
6. **Patient Registration**  
7. **Claim Submission**

These seven domains are essential to smooth financial operations. By evaluating each one, you can remove inefficiencies and improve revenue flow.

---

## 🔧 Professional Services: Implementation

Once your Health Check is complete, you’ll receive a set of implementation options. Your Practice Wellness Expert will:

- Recommend documents, trainings, and consultations
- Schedule sessions tailored to your chosen focus areas

You can selectively implement improvements in any or all of the seven domains.

---

## ✨ Implementation Highlights by Domain

### 🗂 Credentialing  
- Get your providers **in-network faster**  
- Improve **payer relationships** and **negotiated rates**  
- Organize documentation and create welcome materials for insurers

### 💰 Payment Posting  
- Record **all insurance and patient payments** accurately  
- Understand remits, denials, copay changes, and adjustments  
- Ensure **compliant, clean financial records**

### 🧑‍💼 Human Resources  
- Establish consistent hiring and onboarding processes  
- Hire the **right billers/admins**  
- Improve internal workflows and team productivity

### 📋 Eligibility & Benefits  
- Streamline E&B checks  
- Ask the right questions and interpret results  
- Ensure you collect **the right payments from patients**

### 🕒 Aging Follow-Up  
- Maximize collections from **both insurance and patients**  
- Improve appeal processes and communication with payers  
- Track KPIs to measure claim aging and performance

### 📝 Patient Registration  
- Set **clear expectations** around payment and policies  
- Strengthen patient relationships  
- Improve **cash flow consistency**

### 📤 Claim Submission  
- Increase **clean claim rate**  
- Ensure **timely submission**  
- Reduce denials and rejections
