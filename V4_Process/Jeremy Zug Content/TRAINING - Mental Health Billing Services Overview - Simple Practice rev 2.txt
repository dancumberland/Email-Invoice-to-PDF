# 🧾 Mental Health Billing Services Overview  
**Operating in SimplePractice**

---

## 🛠 SimplePractice Billing Services

If you’re already using **SimplePractice**, Practice Solutions will integrate directly into your workflow:

- Add a non-clinical user for the setup specialist
- They will review and configure billing settings
- Ensure EDI and ERA payer connections are properly established

🔐 **HIPAA Compliance:**  
All logins from Practice Solutions are **non-clinical**, meaning they have no access to your notes or confidential clinical data.

### Don't Have SimplePractice?  
Practice Solutions **recommends TherapyNotes** over SimplePractice for billing efficiency and cost-effectiveness. Ask your sales rep for more info if you're open to switching platforms.

---

## 🤝 Your Dedicated Biller

Once setup is complete, you'll be assigned a **US-based billing specialist**. Here's what the workflow looks like:

**Your role in SimplePractice:**
- Add new clients and their information
- Schedule appointments
- Assign CPT codes
- Enter treatment plans and diagnosis codes

**Your biller’s role on bi-weekly billing days:**
- Manage end-to-end billing
- Submit claims
- Post payments
- Follow up on outstanding balances
- Perform eligibility and benefits checks
- Provide general support

Your biller is available during regular business hours for questions and troubleshooting.

---

## 🔧 Services Provided

### ✅ Claim Submission
- In-network, out-of-network, and select EAP claims
- Submitted **electronically** whenever possible for speed and accuracy

### ✅ Claim Follow-Up
- Denied and rejected claims are tracked and followed up on
- Delayed payments from insurance and patients are monitored

### ✅ Eligibility and Benefits Checks
- Performed by request
- Completed within **1–2 business days**
- ⚠️ **Note:** Insurance-provided info is wrong about 30% of the time

### ✅ Payment Posting
- Payments from patients and insurance are recorded directly in **SimplePractice**
- Centralized view of all transactions

### ✅ General Billing Support
- You receive **detailed billing reports** every billing day
- Ongoing access to your dedicated biller for support

## 💸 SimplePractice Billing Pricing

Practice Solutions uses a **tiered per-claim pricing model** based on the number of accepted claims per month:

| Number of Claims | Price per Claim |
|------------------|-----------------|
| 1–25             | $8.75           |
| 26–75            | $7.75           |
| 76–150           | $7.00           |
| 151+             | $6.50           |

- **One-Time Setup Fee:** Starting at **$100** (based on scope of work)
- **Monthly Invoice:** Based on total accepted claims
- ✅ **No hidden fees** — All services are included in the per-claim rate

Included in this rate:
- Claim submission
- Payment posting
- Eligibility and benefits checks
- Claim follow-up
- Personalized support from your biller
