# 🧾 Clean Claim Path  
**How a Claim Results in Payment**

This visual guide walks through the standard process that transforms a clinical visit into an insurance payment. Ensuring each step is completed accurately helps your practice get paid faster and with fewer rejections.

---

## 1️⃣ Progress Note

A claim is created when a **progress note** is written by the rendering provider.  
🔹 This note documents the clinical encounter and provides the necessary CPT codes and diagnoses that justify the claim.

---

## 2️⃣ Submit Claim

After reviewing for accuracy and payer requirements, the claim is submitted—usually via **Electronic Data Interchange (EDI)**—to a **clearinghouse**.  
🔹 This step includes verifying that all required fields are filled, codes are valid, and provider information is correct.

---

## 3️⃣ Clearinghouse QC

The **clearinghouse** reformats the claim and checks for **small data errors** (e.g., invalid NPI numbers, date mismatches).  
🔹 Once cleaned, the claim is forwarded to the insurance payer.  
💡 Think of this as a quality control checkpoint.

---

## 4️⃣ Payer Processing

The **insurance company** processes the claim according to:
- Your credentials (e.g., in-network status)
- The patient’s benefit plan  
🔹 This determines what portion is paid, denied, or marked as patient responsibility.

---

## 5️⃣ Payment

Payment is issued to the provider, or the claim is transferred to the patient for balance due. This is reflected in an **Electronic Remittance Advice (ERA)**.  
🔹 The ERA explains how much was paid, why, and what adjustments (if any) were made.

---

## 📌 Summary Flow

| Step | Name               | Key Action                                                     |
|------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1    | Progress Note      | Provider documents session and codes visit                    |
| 2    | Submit Claim       | Claim submitted via EDI to clearinghouse                      |
| 3    | Clearinghouse QC   | Data validated and cleaned for formatting issues              |
| 4    | Payer Processing   | Insurance adjudicates the claim based on benefits and setup   |
| 5    | Payment            | Payment or denial issued via ERA                              |

---

✅ **Tip:**  
Incomplete or incorrect info at **any stage** of the process may result in delays, denials, or rework. Clean claims = faster revenue.
