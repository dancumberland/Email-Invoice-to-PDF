SOLUTIONS, LLC Anchor: About Practice Solutions, LLC provides mental and
behavioral health billing solutions and services to your practice,
freeing you up to focus on providing best-in-class care to your patients
and growing your practice. We provide professional billing services for
behavior and mental health professionals at a cost-effective rate. Our
priorities are accuracy in filing claims, diligence in collecting on
rejected claims, and showing you progress toward healthy revenue in your
practice. More than anything we strive to make sure there are no
interruptions in your revenue, so you can focus on providing excellent
treatment and care. We welcome business from both group and individual
practices of all tenure. OUR MISSION We exist to empower providers to
focus on patient care by removing the burden of billing and educating
around best revenue cycle management processes. Let\'s break it down; we
want you to feel empowered as a provider in private practice. With
Practice Solutions you have access to the knowledge necessary to make
confident and informed decisions. You can spend as much time with your
patients as possible to utilize your superpower. You can rely on our
knowledge and expertise to do your billing for you, so you don\'t have
to worry about it. Overall, we want to be a tool that you use in your
practice\'s success. OUR TEAM Our billers are all located in the United
States, and are selected based on their depth of experience, their
commitment to helping providers, and their ability to form
relationships. Practice Solutions does the work of finding a quality
biller for you, matching you with someone who will learn the ins and
outs of your practice billing. We have a highly capable team that is
structured to make sure that you have consistent billing service, even
when your biller is out of office. Our team structure allows us to
provide you with the knowledge and experience of several billers for the
price of one. We see ourselves as an extension of your private practice,
working alongside you to make sure that insurance billing is as stress
