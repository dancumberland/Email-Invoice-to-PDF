# LinkedIn Content Style Guide

A practical, copy-and-paste reference for crafting high-performing LinkedIn posts that preserve **Mel Vargasy** while maximizing engagement, reach, and conversion.

---
## 1. Core Post Anatomy
| # | Element | Purpose | Word/Line Targets |
|---|---------|---------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Hook** | Stop the scroll; create curiosity | 1–2 lines, ≤ 28 words total |
| 2 | **Context + Story** | Establish relevance & credibility | 3–6 short paragraphs, 1–2 sentences each |
| 3 | **Value / Insight** | Teach, inspire, or provoke thought | 1–3 paragraphs or lists |
| 4 | **CT​A** | Drive interaction & next step | 1 line question **or** offer |
| 5 | **Hashtags** | Extend reach; clarify theme | 3–5 niche-specific tags |

### 1.1 Copy-Ready Skeleton
```markdown
{HOOK}

{STORY / CONTEXT}

{INSIGHT / LESSON / VALUE}

{CALL-TO-ACTION}

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #brandvoice
```

## 2. Hook Patterns & Formulas
Use one primary pattern per post. Keep hooks **concrete, specific, emotional**.

| Category | Formula | Good Example | Weak Example |
|----------|---------|--------------|--------------|
| Question | *What if* + [desired outcome] *only took* [counter-intuitive effort]? | *What if doubling your leads only took 10 minutes a day?* | *Want more leads?* |
| Statement | Provocative claim that challenges norm | *Cold email is dead—unless you do this.* | *Cold email can work sometimes.* |
| Contrast | *I used to…*, *Now I…* | *I spent $10k on ads. Then I tried this free tool.* | *Ads are expensive, tools help.* |
| Vulnerability | Share flaw → lesson | *I burned out building my first startup—here’s the line I crossed.* | *Burnout happens; take breaks.* |
| Achievement (humble) | Result + credit to process/team | *Hit $40k MRR by deleting half our offers.* | *We’re amazing, hit $40k MRR!* |

---
## 3. Story Development
1. **Situation** – where you/the reader start.  
2. **Struggle** – obstacle, emotion, tension.  
3. **Shift** – insight, decision, or turning point.  
4. **Solution** – what you did.  
5. **Success** – quantifiable or emotional win.  
6. **So What** – takeaway the reader can act on.

> Aim for 120–180 words total. Err on brevity; cut fluff.

---
## 4. Formatting Guidelines
• **Line breaks:** single blank line between every 1–2 sentences.  
• **Paragraph length:** max 2 sentences (≤ 40 words).  
• **Whitespace:** isolate hooks, CTAs, and key lines.  
• **Bullets / numbers:** use "•" or plain "-" bullets; keep to ≤ 5 items.  
• **Emoji:** ≤ 2 per post; place at line start for emphasis (e.g., "🚀").  
• **Bold / CAPS:** use *bold* for one keyword per paragraph; ALL CAPS sparingly for acronyms.

## 5. Transition & Connector Library
Phrase | Use Case
------ | --------
*Here’s the kicker →* | Introduce surprising data
*The real question?* | Pivot to insight
*But there’s a twist…* | Signal contrast
*Fast forward…* | Jump in timeline
*So why share this?* | Lead to takeaway

## 6. Call-to-Action Variations
Objective | Template
--------- | --------
Comments | *Curious—what’s your biggest bottleneck right now?* 
DMs | *DM me “PLAYBOOK” and I’ll send the template.* 
Newsletter signup | *Want weekly AI tips? Comment “AI” and I’ll send the link.* 
Lead magnet | *Grab the checklist (link in first comment).* 
Poll | *Vote below—would this help your team?* 

Place CTA on its **own line**. One CTA per post.

## 7. Content Type Specifics
### 7.1 Personal Story Post
Focus: vulnerability, lesson learned.  
Key: emotional language, first-person.

### 7.2 Teaching / Educational Post
Focus: step-by-step, frameworks.  
Key: numbered lists, clear takeaway.

### 7.3 Reflection Post
Focus: big idea, observation.  
Key: rhetorical questions, invite discussion.

### 7.4 Business Update Post
Focus: milestone, metric.  
Key: humility, gratitude, insight for peers.

### 7.5 Family-Integration Post
Focus: humanize brand, values.  
Key: story about family experience → business principle.

### 7.6 Behind-the-Scenes Post
Focus: process transparency.  
Key: images, lessons from "messy middle".

---
## 8. Language & Voice Patterns
• **Personal Pronouns:** “I”, “we”, “you” to create intimacy.  
• **Voice Modulation:** alternate sentence length; mix short punchy with longer reflective lines.  
• **Emphasis:** italics for nuance, em-dash for pauses, ellipsis sparingly.  
• **Avoid:** jargon, buzzwords (“synergy”), filler (“just”, “really”).

---
## 9. Engagement Boosters
• Start **and** end with a question when seeking comments.  
• Tag **max 2** people/orgs only when directly relevant.  
• Respond to first 10 comments within 60 minutes to spark algorithm.  
• Use *community phrases*: “fellow founders”, “AI builders”.

## 12. Do’s & Don’ts
| ✔️ Do | ❌ Don’t |
|-------|---------|
| Lead with curiosity | Bury hook mid-post |
| Use concrete numbers | Share vague "huge results" |
| Show mistakes & lessons | Humble-brag without insight |
| One clear CTA | Stack multiple asks |
| 3–5 niche hashtags | #TooMany #Generic |
