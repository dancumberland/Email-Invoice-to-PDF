**📋 USAGE:** Copy each prompt below and paste into your AI conversation. Provide the client source materials as specified in each prompt.

**⚡ PREREQUISITE:** Ensure your AI has been initialized using the System Initialization Prompt from `00_START_HERE.md`

## I. Master Brand Voice Guide (`Generated_Brand_Voice_Master.md`)

### Prompt for: `00_How_To_Use_This_Guide_For_AI`

```
Generate a brief (1-2 paragraphs) set of instructions for an AI that will consume this brand voice guide. The instructions should clearly state:
1. The AI's role (e.g., "You are [Brand Name]'s AI voice.").
2. Its primary goal when generating content (e.g., "Your primary goal is to generate content that is [key characteristic 1] while being [key characteristic 2].").
3. A directive to refer to the examples and guidelines within this document.
4. An emphasis on prioritizing authenticity and direct application of the documented patterns.

Keep this section extremely concise and actionable for the consuming AI.
```

### Prompt for: `01_Core_Brand_Identity`

```
Synthesize information from the **collection of summary files in the `Summaries/` directory and relevant raw client content (from `Chunks/` and `Raw/`)** to create the 'Core Brand Identity' section for the NEW Master Brand Voice Guide. The AI should look for recurring themes and explicit statements across all sources to identify the brand's mission, values, personality, USP, and relevant story elements.

Extract and present the following, ensuring comprehensive detail and illustrative examples, aligning with the overall goal of a 20-50 page guide:

*   **Mission:** 1-2 clear, impactful sentences that capture the brand's core purpose.
*   **Core Values:** 3-5 key bullet points. For each value, provide a brief explanatory sentence and, if possible, an example from the source material of how the value manifests in communication.
*   **Brand Essence/Personality:** Identify 3-5 primary descriptive adjectives. For each adjective: 
    *   Provide a detailed definition of what this trait means in the context of the brand.
    *   Include 2-3 distinct, verbatim examples from the source material that vividly illustrate this trait.
    *   Add a brief explanation of how this trait should be conveyed in communications.
*   **Unique Selling Proposition (USP):** 1-2 concise, powerful statements that clearly articulate what makes the brand different or special.
*   **Brand Story/Origin (Conditional):** If the brand's story or origin *directly and significantly* informs its voice and communication style, provide a detailed narrative of 2-4 paragraphs. Highlight elements that shape communication style and include illustrative quotes or anecdotes from source material if available. Otherwise, a brief mention or omission is acceptable if not voice-informing.

Prioritize thorough analysis and rich illustration. The goal is a deep and actionable understanding of the brand's core identity as it pertains to voice, contributing to a comprehensive guide.
```

### Prompt for: `02_Voice_Tone_And_Style`

```
Analyze and synthesize information from **processed client summaries and relevant raw source materials (from `Summaries/`, `Chunks/`, and `Raw/` directories)** focusing on linguistic patterns, vocabulary, tone, stylistic choices, and recurring phrases to create the 'Voice, Tone, And Style' section for the NEW Master Brand Voice Guide. The AI should perform a deep analysis to identify and thoroughly document these elements, aiming for substantial content within the overall 20-50 page guide.

Focus on extracting, defining, and illustrating the following with comprehensive detail and rich examples:

*   **Detailed Voice Characteristics:** Identify and detail 3-5 core voice characteristics (e.g., Warm, Authoritative, Witty, Empathetic, Playful). For each:
    *   Provide a clear definition of what this characteristic means for the brand.
    *   Offer 3-5 distinct, verbatim examples from source material that vividly illustrate this characteristic.
    *   Briefly explain common applications or contexts where this characteristic is particularly important.
*   **Tone Spectrum & Mapping:** Describe the brand's primary tonal range (e.g., from Formal to Informal, Humorous to Serious, Technical to Accessible).
    *   Identify 3-5 key tonal variations or modes the brand uses (e.g., 'Educational & Empowering', 'Challenging & Provocative', 'Supportive & Understanding').
    *   For each tonal variation, explain its purpose, typical contexts, and provide 2-3 examples of it in action. Include guidance on how to select the appropriate tone.
*   **Linguistic Patterns & Stylistic Choices:** Analyze and describe specific, recurring patterns:
    *   Sentence Structure: Typical length (short, long, varied), complexity, common constructions (e.g., active vs. passive, use of questions). Provide examples.
    *   Rhythm and Pacing: Is it fast-paced, measured, conversational, staccato? Provide examples.
    *   Rhetorical Devices: Common use of metaphors, analogies, storytelling, humor, irony, etc., with examples.
    *   Point of View: (e.g., first person, third person, collective 'we'). Provide examples.
*   **Vocabulary & Lexicon (Brand Glossary):**
    *   *Preferred Language:* Compile a comprehensive list of 15-20+ on-brand words, industry-specific jargon (with clear explanations if needed), and characteristic phrases. For each, provide context and 1-2 examples of its typical use from source material.
    *   *Discouraged Language:* Compile a list of 10-15+ off-brand words, clichés to avoid, or terms that misrepresent the brand. Explain why each is discouraged and provide alternatives if applicable. Include examples of misuse if helpful.
*   **Signature Phrases, Taglines & Hook Patterns:**
    *   List all identified signature phrases and taglines with explanations of their meaning, typical usage, and impact. Provide examples.
    *   Document common or effective hook patterns used in content (e.g., starting with a question, a bold statement, a short anecdote, a surprising statistic). Provide 3-5 distinct examples of different hook patterns with brief analysis of why they work for the brand.
*   **Do's and Don'ts:** Create a dedicated sub-section with at least 5-7 clear 'Do' statements and 5-7 'Don't' statements regarding voice and style. Each point MUST be accompanied by a specific, illustrative example (for 'Do's') or a counter-example (for 'Don'ts' showing what to avoid and why).

Strive for depth and clarity, providing enough detail and examples to ensure a robust and actionable understanding of the brand's unique sound and feel.
```

### Prompt for: `03_Content_Strategy_And_Audience`

```
Synthesize information from **processed client summaries and relevant raw content (from `Summaries/`, `Chunks/`, and `Raw/`) related to content strategy, audience research, marketing plans, and engagement data** to create the 'Content Strategy And Audience' section for the NEW Master Brand Voice Guide. The AI should identify and detail core content principles, audience personas, and how the voice applies to content strategy, aiming for substantial content within the overall 20-50 page guide.

Extract, describe, and illustrate the following:

*   **Core Content Themes/Pillars:** List 3-5 primary themes or topics. For each theme:
    *   Briefly describe the theme and its relevance to the brand and audience.
    *   Provide 2-3 example content angles, headlines, or key messages that demonstrate how the brand voice applies to this theme.
*   **Detailed Audience Personas:** For each primary (and key secondary) audience segment:
    *   Develop a persona including demographics (if available/relevant), psychographics, key needs, pain points, motivations, and communication preferences.
    *   Describe in detail how the brand voice should specifically adapt or resonate with this persona, providing 2-3 examples or key considerations for each.
*   **General Approach to Content & Voice Application:** Articulate the overarching philosophy for content (e.g., "Educate and empower through practical, data-driven insights delivered with a confident and approachable voice."). Explain in detail how the voice supports this approach, with examples.
*   **Key Calls to Action (General):** List 3-5 common general CTAs. For each, provide an example of how it's typically phrased in the brand's voice and the strategic intent behind it.

Ensure this section provides a deep, actionable understanding of how content strategy and audience insights inform the practical application of the brand voice.
```

### Prompt for: `04_Key_Exemplars_And_Storytelling`

```
Review **client-provided 'gold standard' content, AI-identified highly representative pieces from processed summaries, and relevant raw content (from `Summaries/`, `Chunks/`, and `Raw/`)** to create the 'Key Exemplars And Storytelling' section for the NEW Master Brand Voice Guide. The AI should select diverse and highly illustrative examples, providing in-depth analysis, aiming for substantial content within the overall 20-50 page guide.

Focus on detailed analysis and diverse examples:

*   **Detailed Exemplar Analysis:** Select 3-5 diverse key exemplar texts (e.g., a blog post excerpt, an email segment, a social media post, a video script snippet). For each exemplar:
    *   Present the verbatim text.
    *   Provide a detailed analysis (2-3 paragraphs) breaking down *how* it embodies the brand voice. Refer explicitly to elements from `01_Core_Brand_Identity` and `02_Voice_Tone_And_Style` (e.g., "Notice the use of [preferred vocabulary] and the [specific tone characteristic] in the second sentence. The hook pattern aligns with [documented hook pattern X].").
    *   Highlight specific word choices, sentence structures, and tonal qualities that make it effective.
*   **Storytelling Frameworks & Archetypes:**
    *   If the brand utilizes specific storytelling frameworks (e.g., Hero's Journey, Problem-Agitate-Solve), describe them in detail and provide 2-3 examples of their application in the brand's content, explaining how voice supports the framework.
    *   If core story archetypes (e.g., Sage, Explorer, Jester) are central to the brand, describe 1-3 key archetypes. For each, explain how it manifests in the voice and provide 2-3 illustrative examples from source material.

The goal is to provide deep, analytical demonstrations of the voice in action, thoroughly deconstructing why and how specific examples are effective.
```

## II. Platform-Specific Add-On Guides (e.g., `LinkedIn_Guide.md`, `Newsletter_Guide.md`)

### General Prompt Structure for ANY Platform-Specific Add-On Guide:

```
You are creating a [Platform Name] Brand Voice Add-On Guide for [Client Name]. This guide will be a **separate, detailed .md file, aiming for 20-50 pages** to ensure comprehensive coverage of platform-specific nuances.

**CRITICAL:** This guide *supplements* the [Client Name] Master Brand Voice Guide. It should briefly **reference** core principles from the Master Guide but primarily focus on **detailing in-depth** the *specific adaptations, structures, examples, and engagement strategies* for [Platform Name]. Avoid extensive repetition of Master Guide content, but ensure this guide provides thorough, standalone understanding for the platform.

Synthesize information by **analyzing ingested raw content (from `Chunks/` or `Raw/`) and their corresponding `Summaries/` files that are specifically tagged or identified as relevant to [Platform Name] or serve as exemplars for it.** The AI should look for and thoroughly document patterns in communication style, content structure, formatting, tonal modulation, and engagement strategies demonstrated in these platform-specific materials.

Structure the [Platform Name]_Guide.md as follows:

# [Platform Name] - Brand Voice Add-On

**Reference:** This guide supplements the [Client Name] Master Brand Voice Guide. Refer to the Master Guide for foundational core identity, voice, and style principles. This document details their specific application and nuance for [Platform Name].

## 1. Purpose & Goals on [Platform Name]
   - Clearly state the primary and secondary objectives for this brand on [Platform Name] (2-3 sentences with brief explanations).

## 2. Content Structure & Format on [Platform Name]
   - Detail common and effective post/content structures (e.g., LinkedIn: specific hook formulas, value proposition structure, multi-point arguments, CTA sequences; Newsletter: engaging subject line strategies, welcome section, main story structure, resource linking, closing remarks). Provide 2-3 full examples of ideal post structures with detailed annotations explaining each part and its voice application.
   - Detail platform-specific formatting nuances (e.g., optimal use of emojis, hashtag strategy and research, video length and style guidelines, image and caption synergy, use of platform features like polls, Q&A, carousels, stories, lives etc.), with 2-3 clear examples for each key formatting point, explaining how it supports the brand voice.

## 3. Tone & Voice Nuances for [Platform Name]
   - Describe in detail any specific shifts, modulations, or emphases in tone for [Platform Name] compared to the general brand voice (e.g., "On LinkedIn, adopt a more data-driven and professionally assertive tone, while maintaining core warmth. This means incorporating more statistics and industry insights, as shown in Example A, B, and C."). Provide 3-4 illustrative examples of these tonal nuances in action.
   - List 3-5 platform-specific Do's and 3-5 Don'ts with clear, actionable examples for each, focusing on voice application unique to the platform. Include counter-examples for the Don'ts.

## 4. Key Exemplar(s) for [Platform Name]
   - Provide 2-3 *detailed, specific, and varied examples* of successful content created by this brand *specifically for [Platform Name]*. These should clearly illustrate the voice, structure, and format adaptations for the platform.
   - For each exemplar, provide a detailed analysis (1-2 paragraphs) similar to the Master Guide exemplar analysis, highlighting platform-specific voice application, structural choices, and why it's effective on [Platform Name].

## 5. Calls to Action (CTAs) & Engagement for [Platform Name]
   - List 3-5 common and effective CTAs used by the brand on [Platform Name], with 1-2 examples for each, showing how they are phrased in the brand voice and tailored to platform conventions.
   - Describe 2-3 key engagement strategies specific to this platform (e.g., "On Instagram, respond to comments within 4 hours using a friendly and appreciative tone, often asking a follow-up question as seen in Example C and D. For DMs, adopt a slightly more informal yet helpful tone as shown in Example E."), providing 2-3 examples of voice in engagement for each strategy.

Ensure this guide is comprehensive, richly illustrated, actionable, and provides a deep understanding of how to effectively use the brand voice on [Platform Name], fitting within the 20-50 page target.
```

**Instructions for AI using these prompts:**
*   Replace `[Client Name]` and `[Platform Name]` as appropriate.
*   When synthesizing information, the AI should primarily draw from the structured data in the **`Summaries/` directory**. For identifying broader context, full exemplars, or details not captured in summaries, the AI should refer to the **original ingested raw content files located in the `Chunks/` or `Raw/` directories**.
*   The process involves analyzing patterns, themes, and specific examples across multiple ingested source materials (transcripts, articles, posts, etc.) to build each section of the Brand Voice Guide.
*   Adhere strictly to the conciseness and example guidelines outlined in `01_AI_INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
