**Document Focus:** ProS Implementation System - Comprehensive Overview of Domains, Categories, and Products

### Comprehensive Summary:

This document provides a complete overview of the "ProS Implementation System," a structured framework designed to assist practices in optimizing their operations. It details the system's architecture, product types, and an exhaustive catalog of products and services offered across seven key operational domains. The system emphasizes customization, process creation, training, and KPI tracking to drive practice efficiency and success.

**I. Implementation System Overview:**
*   **Core Structure:** Comprises **7 Domains** of implementation: Credentialing, Human Resources, Patient Registration, Eligibility & Benefits, Payment Posting, Aging Follow-up, and Claim Submission.
    *   Each domain contains multiple **Categories** of related services/products.
    *   Within categories, **4 Product Types** are offered:
        1.  **Training:** Slide decks providing essential knowledge (customizable).
        2.  **Process Creation:** Templates for creating customized, step-by-step operational processes.
        3.  **Document:** Supporting templates and tools (e.g., checklists, guides) for process creations.
        4.  **Consultation:** Tailored advice or direct assistance with specific tasks, typically billed in time increments.
*   **Purchasing Model:** Products are typically bundled by Category, with Consultations as optional additions. Some products must be sold with others.
*   **Customization:** Many products note levels of customization required (None, Minimal, High).
*   **Key Cross-Domain Products:** Certain tools like the `Claim Management Spreadsheet`, `Insurance Contact Reference`, and `Credentialing Matrix` are foundational and used across multiple domains.
*   **KPIs:** Key Performance Indicators are integral, with products dedicated to defining, tracking (Data Mining, Dashboards), and addressing underperformance in each domain.

**C. Domain: Patient Registration**
    *   **Objective:** Streamlining patient intake and collecting all necessary billing information.
    *   **Intake:** Trainings, process creations, and document templates (Administrative & Patient Intake Packets, CRM consultation) for managing new patient onboarding.
    *   **Provider Assignment & Scheduling:** Tools and processes (Provider Availability Form, Assignment Filter/Log, Scheduling Trainings/Processes, Waitlist) for matching patients to providers and managing appointments.
    *   **HIPAA Compliance:** Training and a comprehensive packet to ensure adherence to HIPAA regulations.
    *   **Checkout:** Trainings, processes, and documents (Payment Authorization Form, Patient Satisfaction Survey, Payment Processor Consultation) for handling post-appointment procedures.
    *   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Consultations, data mining processes, and a dashboard (tracking Provider Utilization, Missed Appointments, Patient Satisfaction, Form Handling Audit) with corrective action strategies.

**D. Domain: Eligibility & Benefits (E&B)**
    *   **Objective:** Verifying patient eligibility and benefits accurately.
    *   **Checks:** Trainings on understanding benefits, performing E&B checks, document templates (E&B Template, Benefit Summary Email), process creations for portal navigation and practice-specific E&B, and an Insurance Contact Reference Guide.
    *   **Prior Authorizations:** Training and process creation for managing prior authorizations.
    *   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Consultations, data mining, a dashboard (tracking Time to Complete E&B, Accuracy, Call Time), and corrective actions.

**E. Domain: Payment Posting**
    *   **Objective:** Efficiently posting payments and logging denials.
    *   **EOB/ERA Receipt:** Trainings on EOB/ERA interpretation, consultations for ERA enrollment and payer portal access, and process creation for portal navigation.
    *   **Posting Payments:** Trainings on EHR payment posting, process creations for specific EHRs and updating patient amounts, and consultation for EFT enrollment.
    *   **Denial Documentation:** Trainings on denied claims, process creation for denial tracking, and a Claim Management Spreadsheet.
    *   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Consultations, data mining, a dashboard (tracking Time to Post Payments, Denial Discovery Time, Accuracy), and corrective actions.

**F. Domain: Aging Follow-up**
    *   **Objective:** Managing outstanding insurance and patient balances, including denial resolution.
    *   **Insurance Aging / Insurance Follow Up:** Trainings and process creations for managing insurance aging reports and denial resolution, plus a Denial Resolution Guide and Payer Communication process.
    *   **Patient Aging Management / Patient Aging Follow Up:** Training, process creation for soft collections, a Patient Aging Management document, and Collection Letter Templates.
    *   **Payer Portals:** Training and process creation for navigating payer portals for claim information.
    *   **Appeals:** Training on the appeals process and an Appeals Packet document.
    *   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Consultations, data mining, a dashboard (tracking Insurance/Patient Aging, Denial Rate, Time to Denial Resolution), and corrective actions.

**G. Domain: Claim Submission**
    *   **Objective:** Optimizing all processes related to submitting claims correctly.
    *   **Note Entry:** Compliance and Coding Guide document.
    *   **Modifier Usage:** Training and a Modifier Usage Guidelines document.
    *   **Claim Information Review:** Training on claim review/submission, process creation for verification, a Note Entry Flyer, and a Rate Increase Notification template.
    *   **Rejection Documentation:** Training on rejection handling, process creation for resolving rejections, and a Rejection Resolution Guide.
    *   **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):** Consultations, data mining, a dashboard (tracking Claim Acceptance Rate, Rejection Discovery/Resolution Time, Pending Notes, First Pass Rate, Time to Claim Submission), and corrective actions.
