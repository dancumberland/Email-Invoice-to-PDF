**I. Introduction to Practice Health Checks (PHC):**
*   **Overview:** A structured audit process (Plan, Interview, Observe, Review, Report, Repeat).
*   **PHC Types:**
    *   **Full PHC:** Covers all 7 domains, min. 2 days, $2000/day.
    *   **Abbreviated PHC (APHC):** Max 3 non-billing domains, max 1 day, variable price. Billing investigations usually require including Credentialing & Patient Registration.
*   **Auditing Principles:** Independence, Objectivity, Competence, Confidentiality.
*   **Audit Techniques:** Interviews, Document Review, Observation, Sampling.
*   **Benefits:** Improved processes, compliance, risk management, enhanced reputation.
*   **Challenges:** Resistance to change, bias, resource needs, interview coaching.

**II. PHC Roadmap & Pre-Engagement Workflow:**
*   **Sales Process:** Managed by Sales team (Capsule CRM for customer/opportunity, contract generation, start date coordination with PWE). PHC Plan Overview Meeting required within 1 week of contract start.
*   **Sales to PWE Handoff:**
    *   **Contract Notification:** Sales sends signed contract to PWE & Accounting.
    *   **PWE Prep (Post-Deposit):** Confirm deposit, create PHC docs in client Google Drive, read contracts, schedule PHC Plan Meeting.
*   **Google Drive Setup:** Standardized folder structure (Service Agreements, PHC Documents with dated subfolder for Packet & Slides, Implementation folder).
*   **QBO Setup:**
    *   **Customer Creation:** Match legal name, input details from Capsule.
    *   **Invoicing:** Create Estimate, then two Invoices: $500 deposit (Due On Receipt), remainder (due 1 business day before PHC).
*   **PHC Plan Meeting:**
    *   Scheduled within 1 week of contract start (delays impact PHC timeline).
    *   Video call preferred. Agenda: introductions, schedule PHC dates (using PHC Plan doc), inform provider of pre-PHC access needs (EHR, portals, CRM).

**III. Performing a PHC - The PHC Packet & Initial Planning:**
*   **PHC Packet:** PWE's guide and the customer-facing deliverable. Sections: Plan, Interview, Observe, Review, Report.
*   **PHC Execution - Plan (Tab 1 of Packet):**
    *   **Scheduling (2-day PHC):** Front-load Day 1 (e.g., 5/7 domains). Presentation in last hour of Day 2. Inform owner of staff needed for Interview/Observe and docs/VPN for Review.
    *   **Interview/Observe Strategy:** Options to combine by category, conduct by role (Management then Admin/Biller), or intersperse with Review. Collaborate with customer for optimal schedule.
    *   **Finalize Plan:** Detail dates, times, required personnel. Communicate who should *not* be in certain interviews for unbiased answers.
*   **PHC Execution - Interview (Tab 2+ of Packet, per domain):**
    *   Follow packet questions for contracted domains.
    *   PWE can ask follow-ups.
    *   Interview right people, one-on-one.
    *   Questions are structured by Domain, Category, PHC stage (Interview, Observe, Review), and Interviewee (Management vs. Admin/Biller).
