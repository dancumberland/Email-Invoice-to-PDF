### Summary:
This first half of the "Therapy for Your Money" podcast features host Julie Harris interviewing Jeremy Zug from Practice Solutions about the intricacies of therapy practices taking insurance. Jeremy emphasizes that taking insurance should align with a therapist's clinical objectives and financial goals, advocating for a strategic approach to selecting payers rather than blanket credentialing. He discusses methods for determining reimbursement rates, such as asking colleagues for ranges or requesting fee schedules from insurers before contracting, stressing the need to start the lengthy credentialing process (6-9 months) early. Key hurdles include choosing an appropriate Electronic Health Record (EHR), ensuring claim submission capabilities, and managing the entire revenue cycle. Jeremy details the revenue cycle from initial patient registration (where accuracy is paramount to avoid future denials) through eligibility verification, claim submission, and payment posting. He strongly advises collecting patient portions (co-pays, deductibles) upfront. The conversation addresses the insurance vs. private pay decision as a "false dichotomy," suggesting practices can strategically do both if it meets clinical, business energy, and financial goals. Jeremy also touches on overcoming challenges like "ghost networks" by having clear systems and focusing on a manageable number of payers, which can still lead to significant referral growth. He introduces Practice Solutions' offerings: educational resources (including his book), a direct billing service, and assistance in building in-house billing departments, all designed to support independent practices. The importance of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for consistent billing, especially in case of biller turnover, is highlighted. Jeremy's book is presented as a comprehensive guide to help therapists understand and navigate insurance complexities, with a special offer for listeners.
