## Part 1 of 1 (Full Document Summary)

**Document Focus:** Outlines the visual branding elements from a 2020 rebrand, including logo variations, color palettes, and typography.

**I. Origin & Core Concept:**
*   **Rebrand Author:** Kate Perry
*   **Core Idea:** "A System of Assets" where "Everything Works Together."

**II. Logo Variations & Applications:**
*   **Main Square Logo:**
    *   Primary logo, used on social media platforms as an identifier.
    *   Can be used in any of the three brand colors, following guidelines.
*   **Main Square Logo With Tagline:**
    *   Primarily for marketing initiatives to educate potential customers (Brand Awareness Campaigns, Social Media Ads, Lead Magnets).
    *   **Restriction:** Should NOT be used with existing clients or where services are already explained/implied.
*   **Main Rectangular Logo:**
    *   Used when a rectangular format is better suited (e.g., header for contracts/legal documents, website logo, social media banners).
*   **The Banner Icon:**
    *   Creates separation in content while building brand awareness.
    *   **Primary Applications:** Website, Presentations, Internal Documents.
    *   Used for Headlines, Article Titles, defining document sections, or as a header/footer.
    *   **Detail:** The Hourglass icon within the banner should always appear white.
*   **The "Sun" Icon:**
    *   Mentioned as a logo variation, but specific application details are not provided in this document.
*   **The Medallion Logo:**
    *   Used to commemorate specific events (e.g., Employee Anniversary, Retreats, Holiday Parties, Special Meetings).
    *   Used to highlight specific types of information (e.g., "Top Tips").
*   *Note: "Hourglass Icon" is also listed initially but its primary described use is within "The Banner Icon."*

**III. Color Breakdown:**
*   **Primary Color:** Navy Blue
    *   Hex: `#1d75bc`
    *   PMS: `7461C`
    *   Thread Colour: `1977 POLYNEON 40` (also listed as `1297` in a duplicate section)
*   **Secondary Color:** Light Blue
    *   Hex: `#27aae1`
    *   PMS: `298 C`
    *   Thread Colour: `1827 POLYNEON 40`
*   **Main Text Color:** Charcoal Grey or black
    *   Hex (Charcoal Grey): `#595959`
    *   **Note:** Logo will never appear in this color, unless a black and white version of the logo is required.
*   **Accent Color:** Lime Green
    *   Hex: `#94c948`

**IV. Recommended Fonts:**
*   **Logo Font:** Poppins Regular
*   **Heading 1 and Article Titles:** Poppins Semi Bold
*   **Main body text and Heading 2+:** Nunito Regular
*   **Exceptions:**
    *   Marketing emails: These fonts are not available; different rules would apply.
    *   Employee emails: Normal fonts in Google Suite (no change).

**Voice Markers:**
*   **Document Tone:** Informative, prescriptive, guideline-oriented.
*   **Implied Author:** Branding lead or designer (Kate Perry noted as rebrander).

**Notable Quotes/Key Phrases:**
*   "A System of Assets"
*   "Everything Works Together"
*   "This logo should not be used with existing clients, nor anywhere where our services are explained or implied" (referring to Main Square Logo with Tagline).
*   "The Hourglass should always appear white in the banner."
