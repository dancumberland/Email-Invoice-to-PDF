## Source File: Podcast - Episode 1 - The Claim Game - Introducing the Board Landscape (Transcript).txt

**Document Focus:** Introduction to "The Claim Game" podcast, its purpose of demystifying Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) for mental health practice owners, and an overview of the six core RCM domains presented as a "game board."

### Comprehensive Summary:
This inaugural episode of "The Claim Game" podcast, hosted by Jeremy and Kathryn Zug of Practice Solutions, introduces the podcast's mission: to simplify the complex world of Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) for mental health practice owners. They frame RCM as a "game" with rules, strategies, and the potential to "win" through optimized financial health for a practice. The episode aims to make RCM less intimidating and more approachable.

Key discussion points include:
*   **Purpose of the Podcast:** To educate practice owners on RCM, making it less scary, less boring, and more understandable, empowering them to evaluate their billing processes effectively, even if they don't manage it themselves.
*   **RCM as a Game Metaphor:** RCM is likened to a game board with rules that can be played well or poorly. This metaphor is used to make the topic more engaging.
*   **The Six RCM "Territories" (Domains):** Jeremy Zug outlines the six core domains of RCM (plus Claim Submission as a distinct final step), which will be explored in detail in future episodes:
    1.  **Credentialing:** The starting point on the game board.
    2.  **Human Resources:** Specifically for billing and administrative staff, crucial for effective RCM.
    3.  **Patient Registration:** Ensuring accurate patient information collection.
    4.  **Eligibility & Benefits (E&B):** Verifying insurance coverage.
    5.  **Payment Posting:** Accurately recording payments.
    6.  **Aging Follow-up:** Managing outstanding claims and patient balances.
    7.  **Claim Submission:** The final step in the process, ensuring claims are sent correctly.
*   **Practice Solutions Origin Story:** Jeremy and Kathryn share how they started Practice Solutions in March 2017, initially helping friends and family, and how their mission evolved to remove the burden of billing and educate providers.
*   **Importance of Understanding RCM:** Emphasizes that practice owners need to understand RCM to know if their billing department is successful, even if they outsource or delegate the work.
*   **Future Topics:** The next episode will cover "Payer Selection."

The overall message is one of empowerment through education, aiming to equip listeners with the knowledge and strategies to master RCM.
