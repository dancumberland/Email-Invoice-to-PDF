**Podcast:** Practice of Therapy Podcast with Gordon Brewer - Session 52
**Guest:** Jeremy Zug, Co-owner of Practice Solutions

### Summary:
This episode of the Practice of Therapy podcast features Gordon Brewer interviewing Jeremy Zug about the intricacies of managing insurance in private mental health practice. The discussion covers deciding whether to join insurance panels, navigating the credentialing process, strategies for out-of-network billing, and the significant benefits of outsourcing these administrative burdens to a specialized company like Practice Solutions.

**Key Discussion Points:**

1.  **Introduction to Jeremy Zug & Practice Solutions:**
    *   Jeremy's background: Started in billing at a Chicago group practice, noticed therapists' aversion to it, and co-founded Practice Solutions to provide expert billing and credentialing services.

2.  **The Decision to Join Insurance Panels:**
    *   **Pros:** Enhances marketing reach and makes therapy more financially accessible to clients.
    *   **Cons:** Can be complex and less lucrative than private pay. It's a misconception that being on numerous panels is essential for practice growth.
    *   **Strategic Selection (Jeremy's "Triple Venn Diagram" Approach):
        1.  **Reimbursement Rates:** Research which panels offer better rates.
        2.  **Ease of Interaction:** Prioritize panels that are less difficult to work with.
        3.  **Referral Volume:** Focus on panels that generate a significant number of patient referrals.
        *   The ideal panels are at the intersection of these three factors.

3.  **Out-of-Network (OON) Billing Strategy:**
    *   A viable alternative to direct paneling.
    *   **Method:** Collect the full cash rate upfront from the patient. Verify their OON benefits (which can be substantial). Submit claims so the patient receives reimbursement directly from their insurer.
    *   This allows the practice to earn its full rate while still enabling patients to use their benefits.

4.  **Navigating the Credentialing Process:**
    *   **Organization is Key:** Maintain meticulous records of all licenses, certifications, work history, etc., for CAQH and other applications.
    *   **Persistence & Follow-Up:** Be prepared for lengthy processing times (up to 180 days for some insurers).
    *   **Closed Panels:** Demonstrating positive treatment outcomes for specific populations can sometimes help gain entry to otherwise closed panels.

5.  **Benefits of Outsourcing to Practice Solutions:**
    *   **Time Savings:** Practice Solutions can save therapists 8-10 hours of administrative work per week, which can be redirected to seeing more clients.
    *   **Expertise & Efficiency:** They handle the entire revenue cycle management: payment posting, claims follow-up, eligibility checks, patient statements, and credentialing.
    *   **Return on Investment (ROI):** The cost of outsourcing is often more than offset by the increased revenue from additional client hours and improved collections.
    *   **Transparency:** Practice Solutions emphasizes keeping practice owners informed about their billing status ("showing their work").

6.  **Importance of Trust and Teamwork:**
    *   When delegating critical tasks like billing, fostering a relationship based on trust with the outsourcing partner is essential.

7.  **How to Connect:**
    *   Website: `practicessol.com`
    *   Specific landing pages for podcast listeners were mentioned.
