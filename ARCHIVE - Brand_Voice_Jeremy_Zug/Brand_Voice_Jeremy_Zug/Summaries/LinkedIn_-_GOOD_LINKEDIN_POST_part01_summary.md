## Summary
This LinkedIn post addresses the challenges private practice owners face when insurance payers, specifically TRICARE during a contractor transition, cause payment disruptions ("go AWOL"). It advises on protecting cash flow by diversifying payer mix, verifying eligibility consistently, maintaining cash reserves, aggressively following up on claims, and educating clients about coverage changes. The post emphasizes that while insurance can be unpredictable, a practice's business strategy shouldn't be, and it invites engagement by asking for readers' strategies in such situations.
