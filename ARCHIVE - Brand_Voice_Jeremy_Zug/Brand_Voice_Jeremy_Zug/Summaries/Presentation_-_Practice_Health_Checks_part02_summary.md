**IV. Performing a PHC - Observe, Review, Report, and Presentation:**

*   **PHC Execution: Observe (Continued):**
    *   **Objective:** Understand processes, collect supporting evidence.
    *   **Method:** PWE observes staff performing tasks (e.g., mock intake). Pause to capture screenshots/images as objective evidence, pasting into "PHC: Transparency Report" tab. Add summary notes.
*   **PHC Execution: Review (Alone):**
    *   **Access:** Requires VPN or direct shared access to client systems (EHR, portals, files).
    *   **Domain-Specific Items:** Detailed lists of documents, system areas, and processes to review for each of the 7 domains (Credentialing, HR, Patient Registration, E&B, Payment Posting, Aging Follow-up, Claim Submission), including KPI Dashboards for each.
*   **PHC Execution: Report Writing:**
    *   **Defining "Done":** Either all packet tasks complete OR clear, high-value discoveries made. Recurrent PHCs address remaining issues.
        *   *Notable Quote:* "Recurrent PHCs are meant to do 2 things: 1) See if the corrective actions following the previous year’s PHC were effective, and 2) drill down into domains that weren’t focused on in the previous year for lack of time."
    *   **Report Structure (using PHC Packet):**
        *   **Title Page:** Practice Name, PS Rep, Date.
        *   **Introduction (pp. 4-6):** Customer/PHC info (Company Overview, Backstory, Objectives, Process, Team, Methodology).
        *   **For Each Domain (pp. 7-58):**
            *   Interview Details (Date/Time, Interviewees, Summary).
            *   Overview (Process: management's view vs. actual; tech/docs needed).
            *   Findings (Use pre-listed "red flags"; customize/remove/add).
            *   Recommendations (Corrective actions for findings; one can cure multiple).
            *   Next Steps: Implementation (Services to sell, ordered by importance).
            *   Index of Objective Evidence (Compile throughout: interview notes, observation/review screenshots. *Emphasis on objective evidence that "speaks for itself."* Often includes "missing" items).
        *   **Conclusion (p. 66):** Overall "health grade," strengths, weaknesses, improvement areas. (Health grade guidelines in development).
*   **Transitioning PWEs:**
    *   Typically, PHC PWE does implementation.
    *   If different, PHC PWE briefs Implementation PWE. Implementation PWE attends Report Summary presentation.
*   **PHC Execution: Presentation:**
    *   Summarizes the written report.
    *   PWE to complete, practice delivery, and prepare for questions.

**V. Selling Implementation & Recurring PHCs:**

*   **Prepare Implementation Options:**
    *   Prioritize findings by impact.
    *   Offer 3 tiers: "Can’t Live Without," "Cherry on Top," "Complete Package."
    *   *Notable Quote:* "Smaller projects for implementation are easier to deliver."
*   **Use Case Studies:** Leverage existing and future case studies.
*   **Working with Sales:** Engage sales team for support if needed.
*   **Implementation Plan:** Outline responsibilities, time/financial investment.
*   **Selling Recurring PHCs:** Focus on effectiveness of prior actions and addressing new/remaining domains.
