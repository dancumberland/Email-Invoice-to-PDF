## Meeting Summary: Aligning on RCM Strategy and Brand Messaging (2025-05-14)

**File:** `Meeting - 20250514 - "Aligning on RCM Strategy and Brand Messaging with Team" - Transcript (1).md`

**Participants:**
*   Dan (Facilitator, AI Development Lead)
*   Jeremy (Practice Solutions Lead/Visionary)
*   Kate (Product Development, Marketing, Content)
*   Catherine (Operations, Structure, Professional Services)
*   Other team members (Various speakers, with Jeremy, Kate, Catherine, and Dan being primary voices)

**Meeting Purpose:**
The primary goal of this meeting was to align on Practice Solutions' Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) strategy, brand messaging, client profiles, and core service offerings. This information is crucial for Dan to develop an AI tool capable of generating content that accurately reflects the company's unique voice and value proposition, supporting the Brand Voice V3 Phase 0 workflow.

**Key Discussion Points & Insights:**

1.  **Client Worldview & Ideal Client Profile (ICP):**
    *   Emphasis on deeply understanding the client's perspective, needs, pain points, and language.
    *   **Billing Services Target:** Primarily mental health private practices.
    *   **Professional Services (Audits, Substack, Podcast):** Broader audience of outpatient private practices across various specialties. The team acknowledged the need to be mindful of industry-specific language (e.g., "40 patient hours a week" has different implications depending on the specialty).

2.  **The "Seven Domains of Billing":**
    *   A core framework for Practice Solutions' RCM audits and services, crucial for understanding a practice's health.
    *   **Domains Identified:**
        1.  Credentialing
        2.  Contracting
        3.  Eligibility & Benefits Verification
        4.  Claim Submission
        5.  Payment Posting
        6.  Aging Follow-up
        7.  Human Resources (specifically, hiring and training quality billing/admin staff to run these processes) - This was highlighted as the seventh domain, sometimes visually or conceptually distinct but integral to the overall RCM health.

3.  **Core Value Proposition & Differentiators:**
    *   **Transparency:** Actively working against the "black hole" phenomenon often experienced by practices that outsource billing. Practice Solutions aims to provide clients with regular, detailed updates on outstanding claims.
    *   **Proactive Follow-up:** Diligently pursuing denied claims, countering the common client assumption that a denied claim means permanently lost revenue.
    *   **Education:** Empowering clients through various content channels (Substack, podcasts) and direct consultation as part of their service offerings.
    *   **Systemization:** Focusing on building robust, reliable systems for RCM rather than relying on ad-hoc processes or individual heroics.

4.  **Brand Voice & Team Dynamics:**
    *   The distinctive Practice Solutions brand voice is significantly shaped by the dynamic interplay between Jeremy and Catherine.
    *   **Jeremy:** Characterized as visionary, energetic, fast-paced, and the source of innovative ideas.
    *   **Catherine:** Brings structure, linear thinking, operational excellence, and a tempering influence to Jeremy's pace, ensuring detailed execution and grounding.
    *   This complementary pairing is considered their "sweet spot" and is essential to the Practice Solutions identity. The AI tool must aim to capture this balanced, nuanced voice.
    *   The team values open disagreement and collaborative problem-solving as part of their effective working dynamic.

5.  **AI Tool Development & Content Strategy:**
    *   The initial version of the AI tool will serve as a general-purpose engine designed to capture the core brand voice derived from these discussions.
    *   Future development may include more specialized "machines" tailored for specific platforms (e.g., "Jeremy's LinkedIn content production machine") or particular types of content, layering platform-specific nuances on top of the foundational brand voice.
    *   The AI will assist in repurposing existing content and generating new, targeted materials that align with the brand.

6.  **Action Items & Next Steps:**
    *   Kate will provide Dan with several key documents to inform the AI's training:
        *   Detailed descriptions of services (e.g., Practice Health Check, implementation services).
        *   Professional training materials used by the team.
        *   Examples of technical language, client communications, and positive client feedback (e.g., Google reviews).
        *   Podcast transcripts, particularly those that effectively showcase the Jeremy/Catherine dynamic.
    *   Dan anticipates having a V1 of the AI tool ready for the team to experiment with in approximately two weeks.
