## Summary
This transcript segment captures the beginning of a kickoff meeting between Dan and Jeremy (Speaker 2). They discuss initial administrative points (contract payment terms) before diving into Jeremy's marketing challenges. Jeremy explains his company (35 employees, primarily billers) recently demoted their marketing lead due to a lack of tangible sales results, despite her creativity and marketing degree. The core marketing goal is to generate 50-70 warm leads per month for the sales team. The company's mission is twofold: to empower providers by removing billing burdens (done-for-you service) and to educate on best RCM practices. To support the latter, they are launching a podcast and a Substack ("The Claim Game"). Jeremy sees content marketing as key but admits it's underdeveloped. Dan proposes his AI-powered content creation tools as an "engine" to streamline processes (blog posts, newsletters, podcast scripts), making it feasible for a capable executive assistant, rather than a specialized marketer, to manage with proper direction. Dan also offers to build a content calendar tool and connect Jeremy with an agency for ads and podcast editing. Jeremy notes his current EA is going on maternity leave soon.
