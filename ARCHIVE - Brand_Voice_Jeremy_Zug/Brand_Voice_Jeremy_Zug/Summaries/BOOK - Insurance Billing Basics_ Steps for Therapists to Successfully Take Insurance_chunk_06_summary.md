# Summary of "BOOK - Insurance Billing Basics_chunk_06" (Lines 1062-1261)

## 1. Summary Overview:
This sixth chunk of "Insurance Billing Basics" covers the entirety of **Chapter 6: Eligibility and Benefit Checks (E&B Checks)**. This chapter is crucial for understanding how to verify a patient's insurance coverage and financial responsibility before or at the time of service, which is vital for minimizing billing errors and ensuring payment.

The chapter is structured as follows:

1.  **The Difference Between Eligibility and Benefits (Lines 1066-1076):**
    *   **Eligibility:** Confirms if a patient has a valid insurance policy that can be used for mental health services.
    *   **Benefits:** Details the specific coverage under the plan (e.g., types of services covered, limitations, reimbursement amounts).
    *   **Verification of Benefits (E&B Check):** The process of confirming these details with the insurer, ideally during pre-registration, to understand deductibles, copayments, and other out-of-pocket expenses.
    *   **Insurance Plan Year:** The 12-month period for benefit calculation, which may not align with the calendar year. Deductibles and maximums reset at the start of each plan year.

2.  **How to do an E&B Check (Lines 1078-1172):**
    *   **What to Know Before You Call:**
        *   Have correct insurer contact information (provider-specific E&B phone numbers or portal details).
        *   Be prepared with patient information (name, DOB, address, insurance card details) and your NPI/Tax ID.
        *   Block out sufficient time (at least an hour) due to potential hold times.
    *   **What Information to Collect or Record (Sample E&B Template Provided - Lines 1106-1126):**
        *   Date of check & who completed it.
        *   Plan Reset Date and Frequency.
        *   Active Insurance (Y/N) - stop if not active and get updated info.
        *   Telehealth Coverage (Y/N).
        *   Authorization Needed (Y/N) for common CPT codes (e.g., 90791, 90837, 90834) and any others you use.
        *   **Patient Responsibility:**
            *   Individual & Family Deductible (total and accumulation, e.g., $120/$500).
            *   Copay amount.
            *   Coinsurance percentage.
            *   Individual & Family Out-of-Pocket Maximum (total and accumulation).
        *   Reference number for the call and representative's name.
    *   **Key Phrases:** Use "outpatient mental health benefits" when speaking to a representative.
    *   **Best Practice:** Perform a full phone E&B check once per plan cycle (usually annually), with follow-up portal checks before each session for plan activity and accumulations.
    *   **Want Help?** Mentions services like Practice Solutions for E&B checks.

3.  **How to Interpret Eligibility and Benefit Information (Lines 1174-1248):**
    *   Crucial for providing **Good Faith Estimates** (No Surprises Act).
    *   Uses a hypothetical allowed amount of $100 for a 90791 session for examples.
    *   **Patient Responsibility Phases (Diagram mentioned - Line 1181):**
        *   **Patient Owes a Deductible:** Patient pays the full contracted rate until the deductible is met. (Example provided: $156.34/$500 deductible means patient owes $100).
        *   **Patient is Cost Sharing With Insurance:** Occurs after deductible is met or if plan waives deductible for mental health.
            *   **Owes a Copay:** Patient pays a fixed dollar amount (e.g., $25 copay means patient owes $25, insurance pays $75).
            *   **Owes a Coinsurance:** Patient pays a percentage of the allowed amount (e.g., 20% coinsurance on $100 allowed amount means patient owes $20, insurance pays $80).
        *   **Patient is Fully Covered by Insurance:** Occurs after out-of-pocket maximum is met. Patient owes $0, insurance pays the full $100.

4.  **Chapter Summary (Lines 1250-1252):** Emphasizes the importance of E&B checks for limiting billing errors and the time-consuming nature, suggesting outsourcing if it becomes a burden.

5.  **Follow-Up Actions to Take (Lines 1254-1261):** Includes collating E&B resources, developing a consistent E&B process, saving E&B info in EHR, reviewing with patients, and following up on authorization requests.
