**Document Focus:** A comprehensive internal guide detailing the brand's voice, tone, content creation guidelines, platform-specific strategies, and extensive keyword lexicons.

**I. Brand Voice & Tone Definition:**
*   **Voice Spectrum:**
    *   Slightly Mass Appeal (vs. Elite)
    *   Gently Playful (vs. Serious)
    *   Midpoint Conventional/Rebel
    *   Slightly Friend (vs. Authority)
    *   Closer to Young & Innovative (vs. Mature & Classic)
*   **Core Description:** "Empathetic to the provider’s feelings. We are an approachable friend empowering providers to make the best decisions for their practice. We know how to have fun while demonstrating expert smarts."
*   **Characteristics:** Approachable, Smart, Empathetic, Fun, Empowering (with examples for each).

**II. Content Creation Guidelines - Actionable Tips:**
*   **Analogies:** DO use; DON'T convolute or use multiple in one post.
*   **Pop Culture References:** DO use relevantly with hashtags; DON'T overuse.
*   **Affiliate Mentions:** DO reference when appropriate.
*   **Internal Linking:** DO link to `practicesol.com` for added value using keywords/titles; DON'T use generic link text like "click here."
*   **Political Policy:** DO examine non-partisanly; DON'T pick sides.

**III. "Thumbs Up" - Best Practices:**
*   Unified analogies, appropriate affiliate references, internal links.
*   Relevant images and hashtags (extensive list provided, e.g., #Insurance, #RevenueCycleManagement, #PrivatePractice, #MentalHealthParity).
*   End posts with a question, respond to comments.
*   Provides examples of good/bad blog posts and a good LinkedIn post with links.

*   **Instagram:** Relational, emotional. Emojis.
*   **LinkedIn:** Conversational, engage in discourse on current events. Emojis.
*   **Facebook:** Conversational. Emojis.
*   **Substack (Jeremy):** (No specific tone listed, but implied to be distinct)

**VI. Top 10 LinkedIn Posts Analysis (Examples):**
*   Focus on politically relevant/charged topics (prior authorizations).
*   Use concise, emotional language, listicle format (denied claims).
*   Address current, emotionally charged events (Tricare issues).

**VII. Blog Posting Strategy:**
*   **For Google Searchers:** Build brand awareness, resolve problems, build trust, keep them reading (subscribe), position as a solution.
*   **For Subscribers:** Further develop trust, current event material.
*   **For Social Media Sharing:** Get views, shares, and conversations.
