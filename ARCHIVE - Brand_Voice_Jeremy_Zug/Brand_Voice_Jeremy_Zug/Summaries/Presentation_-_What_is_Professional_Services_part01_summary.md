## Source Document Review & Summary: Presentation - What is Professional Services_.txt

### Overall Summary:
This presentation defines Practice Solutions' "Professional Services" offering, a new way to serve customers by providing expert guidance to maximize revenue, reduce administrative costs, and resolve billing pain points. It details the value proposition, the two main components (Practice Health Checks and Implementation Projects), ideal client profiles, and the cyclical workflow from initial Practice Health Check (PHC) through to implementation and follow-up PHCs. The ultimate goal is to equip practices with best-in-class Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) practices for sustained success.

### Key Sections and Insights:

**I. Value Proposition of Professional Services:**
*   **Core Goals:**
    *   Ensure maximization of revenue.
    *   Reduce administrative costs (e.g., by streamlining or enabling in-house billing).
    *   Understand underlying causes of billing pain points through investigation.
    *   Identify unknown areas of RCM concern.
    *   Measure practice success year-over-year.
    *   Implement pre-developed, adaptable processes under expert guidance.
*   **Mechanism:** Leverages Practice Solutions' knowledge and expertise to help practices achieve well-functioning billing and administrative departments, aligning with the mission to empower providers to focus on patient care.

**II. How Value is Provided (Two Core Components):**
1.  **Practice Health Checks (PHCs):**
    *   **Nature:** Thorough investigation (audit-like) of the practice's RCM, involving interviews, observation, and system/document review.
    *   **Deliverables:** Overall practice health score, detailed findings, and recommendations for improvement.
    *   **Purpose:** Can be performed regularly to measure improvement.
    *   **Timeline:** A few hours to several days (averaging two days for investigation).
2.  **Implementation Projects:**
    *   **Nature:** Practical application/consulting phase following a PHC.
    *   **Process:** Provider reviews PHC recommendations and can purchase implementation categories (training, process creation, documents, consultation) to address findings.
    *   **Goal:** Install lasting systems for improvement, guided by a Practice Workflow Engineer (PWE), to achieve billing excellence.

**III. Ideal Client Scenarios for Professional Services:**
*   Practices managing over $1M in revenue.
*   Those with more complex billing requirements.
*   Practices transitioning to an in-house biller.
*   Those fighting a large insurance aging project.
*   **Rationale for PHC First:** A PHC is crucial to diagnose issues accurately before prescribing solutions (implementation). It helps prioritize, uncover true needs vs. perceived needs, achieve results faster, and prove value.
    *   *Exception:* Current insurance billing clients might be offered implementation without a preceding PHC.

**IV. The "E.E.P.A.R.R." Model for Implementation Value:**
*   **Educate:** Staff on how RCM *should* function (via trainings).
*   **Equip:** Practices to implement Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) with RCM best practices (via Process Creations).
*   **Provide:** Tools for ease of process execution (via documents, KPI Dashboards).
*   **Assist:** With strategy and issue resolution during adjustment to new systems (via consultation).
*   **Recover & Reduce (The Result):** Recover aging revenue and reduce costs due to inefficiency.

**V. Billing and Administrative Domains Covered:**
*   **Administrative Domains:** Credentialing, Human Resources, Patient Registration.
*   **Billing Domains:** Eligibility & Benefits, Payment Posting, Aging Follow-Up, Claim Submission.

**VI. Professional Services Workflow (Cyclical Model):**
1.  **PHC Sales:** Potential clients start here. Sales establishes PHC type (full or abbreviated).
2.  **PHC Execution:** PWE conducts PHC (plan, interview, observe, review), compiles Transparency Report & Summary Presentation.
3.  **Post-PHC Client Paths:**
    *   **Path 1 (Internal Improvement):** Client uses report to improve internally, schedules follow-up PHC (6-12 months).
    *   **Path 2 (Implementation):** Client moves to Implementation Sales; PWE + Sales curate product package.
    *   **Path 3 (No Further Action):** Least favorable, unless practice is already optimized.
4.  **Implementation Sales & Execution:**
    *   If project lost: End journey or return to PHC Sales.
    *   If project won: PWE develops fulfillment schedule, customizes/delivers products.
5.  **Post-Implementation:** Client returns to PHC Sales for follow-up PHC to measure effectiveness.

**VII. Overarching Goal:**
*   "A collaboration for Practice Success."
*   Equip practices with the best RCM practices through investigation, objective evaluation, and implementation.
