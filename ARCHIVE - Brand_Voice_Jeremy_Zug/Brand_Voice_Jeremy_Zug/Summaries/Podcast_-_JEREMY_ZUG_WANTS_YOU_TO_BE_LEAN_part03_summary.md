**Podcast:** Practice of the Practice with Joe Sanok – Session Number 297
**Guest:** Jeremy Zug, Co-owner of Practice Solutions

### Summary:
This concluding segment focuses on the practical application of LEAN by identifying and eliminating waste, strategies for implementing change, and summarizing the core areas where LEAN benefits private practices.

**Key Discussion Points:**

1.  **Value-Add Criteria & Waste Identification:**
    *   Jeremy reiterates the **3 criteria for a process to add value in healthcare:**
        1.  The patient must care about it.
        2.  The activity changes the patient or knowledge about them (e.g., eligibility checks).
        3.  It must be done correctly the first time (avoiding "rework").
    *   **Common Wastes in Private Practice:**
        *   **Transportation/Movement:** Unnecessary physical travel (e.g., to a distant, underperforming office) or digital disorganization (wasting time searching for files).
        *   **Rework/Repeating Steps:** Jeremy uses Practice Solutions' streamlining of secondary claims submission (from 15 to 9 steps) as a prime example of eliminating redundant actions and optimizing workflow.
        *   **Wasted Time (Lack of Focus/Multitasking):** While using small pockets of time is good, Jeremy advises against multitasking within core work processes, advocating for focused, uninterrupted task completion (batching similar tasks, as Joe suggests, is a good strategy).

2.  **Implementing LEAN Changes:**
    *   **Overcoming Resistance:** Acknowledge that change can be daunting but LEAN changes are aimed at positive improvements.
    *   **"Think FISH" (Focus, Improve, Sustain, Habit - implied):** Tackle one problem area at a time. Don't try to fix everything at once. Example: If improving accounting, focus solely on receipt tracking initially.
    *   **Process:** Improve the chosen area, sustain the new method until it's ingrained, reward the success, and then continuously seek further improvements.
    *   **Monitor & Adjust:** Constantly evaluate: Did the change work? How much time was saved? If not, why?

3.  **Core Applications of LEAN in Private Practice (Jeremy's View):**
    *   **Patient Processes:** Intake, payment, EHR interaction, billing flow.
    *   **Admin Processes:** Revenue cycle management, interface between billing and other admin tasks.
    *   **Marketing Processes:** Optimizing outreach and client acquisition.
    *   **Billing Processes:** A key area for Practice Solutions, focusing on first-time payment accuracy and efficient staff workflows.
    *   The value of **outsourcing** is re-emphasized for tasks that experts can perform more efficiently.

4.  **Conclusion & Resources:**
    *   Joe Sanok highlights the main takeaway: the need for systems, good people, and eliminating inefficiencies ("cut the fat").
    *   Listeners are directed to `practiceofthepractice.com` for more resources and to `practiceofthepractice.com/fun` to connect with Jeremy Zug at Practice Solutions.

This final part provides actionable strategies for private practitioners to implement LEAN thinking, focusing on incremental improvements and the significant benefits of optimized processes.

### Notable Quotes:
*   Jeremy Zug (on value criteria): "The activity has to be run correctly the first time. Often, in the manufacturing world, it’s called ‘rework’... That’s a ton of waste."
