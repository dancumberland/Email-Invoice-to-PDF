**II. Main Content (Markdown Sections):**

*   **Introduction (Section: 🔄 6 Domains of Revenue Cycle Management (RCM))**
    *   The document explains that the graphic (and descriptions) illustrate the essential stages of a complete billing and administrative cycle, foundational for efficient revenue collection.

*   **Foundational RCM Domains:**
    *   **➤ Credentialing:** Enrolling providers with insurance panels. Called "the first gate to ensure your services are reimbursable."
    *   **➤ Patient Registration:** Accurate intake of patient demographics, insurance, and consents to ensure valid claim submission.

*   **Core Billing Cycle (Cyclical Process):**
    *   **🔁 Eligibility & Benefits (E&B):** Verifying patient coverage *before* service (co-pay, deductible, plan limits).
    *   **📤 Claim Submission:** Preparing and submitting claims with precise coding and payer-specific formatting.
    *   **📈 Aging Follow-Up:** Tracking unpaid claims, managing resubmissions, appeals, and payer communications.
    *   **💵 Payment Posting:** Recording payments and adjustments in the EHR for accurate balances and reporting.

*   **Summary Flow (Section: 📌 Summary Flow)**
    *   A `mermaid` flowchart visually depicts the RCM flow:
        *   Linear Start: `Credentialing` → `Patient Registration`
        *   Cyclical Core: `E&B` → `Claim Submission` → `Aging Follow-Up` → `Payment Posting` → (loops back to) `E&B`.
    *   This highlights that after initial setup (Credentialing, Patient Registration), the core billing activities form a continuous loop.
