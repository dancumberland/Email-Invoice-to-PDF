This first segment of the podcast introduces Jeremy Zug and the core topic: applying LEAN principles to private practice. Host Joe Sanok begins by emphasizing the importance of processes and systems for productivity, using Michael Hyatt's 'on stage, back stage, off stage' analogy.

**Key Discussion Points:**

1.  **Jeremy Zug & Practice Solutions Update:**
    *   Jeremy, co-owner of Practice Solutions, returns to the podcast.
    *   Practice Solutions has grown, now serving over 150 clinicians and expanding their team.
    *   A significant new service offered is **credentialing and paneling**, addressing a major need for clinicians.
    *   **Value of Outsourcing Billing:** Jeremy highlights that transitioning billing to Practice Solutions is quick (2-3 weeks) and affordable. It's not just about cost-saving but often about revenue recovery, as specialized billers can capture income that might otherwise be missed. Joe Sanok frames outsourcing as a "multiplier," freeing up clinicians' time for core, income-generating activities.

2.  **Introduction to LEAN Methodology:**
    *   Joe Sanok shares his initial exposure to LEAN principles.
    *   **Jeremy's Definition of LEAN:** "Cycle time reduction through waste elimination." The core idea is to identify and remove non-essential activities from processes to achieve maximum efficiency.
    *   **Origins of LEAN:** The methodology originated in manufacturing (notably Toyota in Japan) where it helped companies increase production quality and quantity while reducing waste and inventory. American companies later adopted it.
    *   **Applicability to Service Industries:** Jeremy notes that LEAN has been applied to service industries, including private practice, for the past 10-15 years and is highly viable for improving operations.

This section sets the context for a deeper dive into how private practices can benefit from LEAN principles by streamlining their operations and eliminating inefficiencies.
