*   **Introduction (Section: 🧾 Helping Your Patients Understand Insurance)**
    *   Explains the guide's purpose: to illustrate the division of financial responsibility between patient and insurer across three billing stages, aiming for clearer conversations.

*   **The Three Stages of Insurance Financial Responsibility:**
    1.  **🏁 Stage 1: Paying the Deductible (Gate 1: Meeting the Deductible)**
        *   **Patient Pays:** 100% (unless deductible is waived or doesn't apply for mental health benefits).
        *   **Insurance Pays:** 0% (correspondingly).
        *   *Key Point (💡):* Patient is fully responsible until the deductible amount is met.

    2.  **🤝 Stage 2: Cost Sharing with Copay or Coinsurance (After Deductible)**
        *   **💵 Copay Model:** Patient pays a fixed amount (e.g., $25) per session; insurance pays the rest of the contracted rate.
        *   **📊 Coinsurance Model:** Patient pays a percentage (e.g., 30%) of the contracted rate; insurance pays the remaining percentage.
        *   *Key Point (💡):* This stage is often confusing for patients who might expect full coverage but are still responsible for a portion.

    3.  **🛡️ Stage 3: 100% Insurance Coverage (Gate 2: Meeting the Out-of-Pocket Max)**
        *   Once the patient's annual out-of-pocket maximum is met:
        *   **Patient Pays:** 0%.
        *   **Insurance Pays:** 100% (for the remainder of the year, subject to plan limits/network status).
        *   *Key Point (💡):* Insurance covers the full cost after this threshold.
