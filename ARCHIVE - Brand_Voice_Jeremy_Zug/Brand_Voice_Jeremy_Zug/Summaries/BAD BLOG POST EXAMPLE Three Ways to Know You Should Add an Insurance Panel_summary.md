## Summary
This blog post outlines three key variables for therapists to consider before joining an insurance panel: aligning panel choices with their ideal client population, evaluating the financial implications of varying reimbursement rates, and assessing their available time for the increased administrative workload. It emphasizes making a deliberate and thoughtful decision to ensure practice sustainability. The post concludes by offering a consultation to discuss these decisions further.
