## Summary
This document details three buyer personas for a mental health billing service, targeting different practice sizes and needs. "Cash Flow Cathy" is a solo practitioner ($100K+ revenue) struggling with billing complexity, aging A/R, and software inefficiencies, seeking efficiency and transparent collaboration. "Growth-Minded Greg" leads a small group (2-4 providers, $300K+ revenue) facing operational complexity and inconsistent cash flow, looking for scalable billing solutions and financial clarity. "Systems-Focused Sam" heads a medium group (4-8 providers, $500K+ revenue) needing standardized workflows, AR reduction, and high-level financial oversight for predictable, scalable operations.
