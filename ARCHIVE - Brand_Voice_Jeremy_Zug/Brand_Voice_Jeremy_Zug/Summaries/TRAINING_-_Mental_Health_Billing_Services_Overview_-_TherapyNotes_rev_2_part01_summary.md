*   **Section 1: 🛠 TherapyNotes Billing Services**

    *   **Existing TherapyNotes Users:** Client adds a Practice Solutions user for setup specialist to configure settings, EDI, and ERA.

*   **Section 2: 🤝 Your Dedicated Biller**
    *   A personal, US-based biller is assigned after setup.
    *   **Client's Role (in TherapyNotes):** Manages client intake, scheduling, CPT coding, treatment plans, and diagnoses.
    *   **Biller's Role (bi-weekly billing day):** Handles all described billing processes.

*   **Section 3: 🔧 Services Provided**
    *   **Claim Submission (✅):** All claims (in-network, out-of-network, select EAP) submitted electronically.
    *   **Claim Follow-Up (✅):** Tracks and follows up on denied, rejected, or unpaid claims; monitors payment timelines.
    *   **Eligibility & Benefits (E&B) Checks (✅):** By request, 1-2 business day turnaround. *Important Note (⚠️):* "Insurance companies provide incorrect information roughly **30% of the time**..."
    *   **Payment Posting (✅):** All payments recorded directly in TherapyNotes for centralized financial data.
    *   **General Billing Support (✅):** Billing activity report provided every billing day; biller available during business hours.

*   **Section 4: 💰 Pricing Overview**
    *   Two billing options offered:
        1.  **📦 Base Billing Package:**
            *   **Setup Fee:** Starting at $100 (based on effort).
            *   **Monthly Invoice:** **6% of gross practice revenue** (includes insurance reimbursements, copays, deductibles, and private-pay).
            *   **Additional Charges:** Postage (if applicable).
        2.  **📦 Billing with Patient Support:**
            *   **Setup Fee:** Starting at $100.
            *   **Monthly Invoice:** **6% of collected insurance revenue** (includes insurance reimbursements, copays, and deductibles only).
            *   **Additional Charges:** Postage, plus any requested legacy data migration.
    *   **Tip (🧾):** The "Base Billing Package" is recommended for comprehensive support including all payments (private pay). The "Billing with Patient Support" package is more narrowly focused on insurance-related revenue.
