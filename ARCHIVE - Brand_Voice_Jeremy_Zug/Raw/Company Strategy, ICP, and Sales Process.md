# **Buyer Personas**

# **Established Practice \- Solo doing at least $100K**

## **Persona: Cash Flow Cathy**

**Overview**

Cash Flow Cathy is an established solo practitioner who has built her practice to a steady six-figure revenue. She has a full caseload and is committed to maintaining a client-centered experience. Cathy has a blend of government and private insurance payers with a handful of out-of-pocket clients. 

**Common Problems and Needs**

* Under Billing Contract: Cathy may work with another billing service but wants out.  
* Billing Complexity: Cathy is facing time constraints due to her mixture of insurance payers and out-of-pocket services. She has started to experience claim issues that have led to a buildup of her aging. Claims submission and payment reconciliation have become overwhelming and frequently become a “back-burner” task.   
* Insurance Aging Issues: Cathy’s practice sees growing A/R, which has led to cash flow issues. She is not collecting every dollar she could be but doesn’t know how to do that herself.   
* Software Inefficiencies: Cathy uses various software (EHR, Email, Portals) but could be more organized and coordinated in how she uses them and in organizing her access information to these systems.   
* Revenue Optimization Concerns: Cathy wants to maximize revenue but doesn’t always feel she has the money to spend on administrative overhead.    
* 

**Thinking and Strategy**

Cathy sees her practice as a streamlined solo operation with the potential for greater efficiency rather than adding more staff or expenses. She is not gifted analytically and appreciates transparent collaboration. Cathy wants to know the billing is working but doesn’t want to do it herself. Cathy may have had a bad experience with outsourcing and is having trouble seeing that she has a lousy vendor. 

**Who Cathy Is**

Cathy sees herself as a therapist but may not see herself as a business owner. She values:

* Efficiency and Precision: Cathy appreciates working with people who are clear communicators and can keep her informed. She values efficiency because she is very cost-conscious.   
* Strategic Insight: She enjoys understanding the “why” behind the process.  
* High-Level Administration: She is capable but prefers delegating technical tasks, especially around billing, if it means maintaining client relationships. 

Cash Flow Cathy could benefit from a Practice Health Check to identify systemic billing issues and use the transparency report to understand her aging. You will likely need to sell value-based pricing on aging. She will likely benefit more and for longer outsourcing her billing to Practice Solutions until she grows. Outsourcing will simplify her practice management, align with her growth goals, and enhance her patient experience.

# **Small Group \- 2-4 doing at least $300K**

## **Persona: Growth-Minded Greg**

**Overview**

Growth-Minded Greg leads a small group practice that’s newly established and growing, generating around $300K in annual revenue. Greg has built a team of 2-4 providers who share a commitment to client-centered care, and he’s now focused on refining the practice’s operational processes to support further growth. Greg’s practice has encountered increased organizational complexity with multiple providers, especially given their mix of insurance panels.

**Common Problems and Needs**

* Increased Operational Complexity: Managing claims and reimbursements across multiple providers and a blend of insurance plans creates administrative bottlenecks.  
* Inconsistent Cash Flow: Greg is experiencing inconsistent cash flow due to a backlog of aging claims and insurer payment delays.  
* Limited Technology Integration: The practice’s billing software may not correctly reflect provider information, leading to inefficient processes and data inaccuracies.  
* Desire for Financial Clarity: Greg needs clear financial visibility to make strategic decisions, accurately forecast the practice’s cash flow, and pinpoint opportunities for revenue growth.

**Thinking and Strategy**

Greg is future-focused and becoming process-oriented. He seeks growth strategies that will scale with the practice as he brings on more providers. He understands the value of outsourcing administrative tasks to people who can do them better than he can. Greg wants to trust whoever he has doing billing, whether he outsources or hires. 

**Who Greg Is**

Greg starts viewing himself as a business owner but still maintains a caseload. He is becoming process-oriented and wants to work on the business, not just in the business. His strengths include:

* Big-Picture Thinking: Greg is always looking to improve efficiency and grow the practice’s capacity.  
* Accountability and Structure: Greg values organized solutions and expects accountability in all operational processes.  
* Delegation with Insight: While he is hands-on, Greg prefers empowering others with the right tools and technology so he can focus on expanding the practice’s reach and influence.

Growth-Minded Greg can benefit from a Practice Health Check to gain actionable insights on his billing but may need more money to pay for an optimization project. He may benefit more from outsourcing his billing to a partner who will strategically work with him to build his billing department. He values partners who can streamline billing workflows and set up scalable systems that will grow with his practice, keeping cash flow healthy and predictable.

# **Medium Group- 4-8 doing at least $500K**

## **Persona: Systems-Focused Sam**

**Overview**

Systems-Focused Sam heads a medium-sized group practice with 4-8 providers, generating over $500K annually. She is a leader who establishes scalable systems and processes to support a more significant operation without sacrificing efficiency. Her practice includes a more focused population of clients or treatment modalities with a focused credentialing strategy. The practice has grown where cash flow, consistent reimbursements, and streamlined workflows are critical for sustainability.

**Common Problems and Needs**

* Operational Inefficiencies: Sam’s practice faces challenges in standardizing and coordinating billing workflows with multiple providers, leading to gaps and inconsistencies in either cash flow or payroll.   
* High AR and Claim Aging: Sam is concerned about the high volume of aged claims impacting cash flow and needs processes to bring down AR across providers.  
* Fragmented Technology Solutions: Sam’s current billing software is satisfactory for their needs as a group, but she needs more consistent SOPs or a reliable billing company.   
* Financial Oversight and Projections: She wants to understand how to predict the practice's likely revenue growth and wants high-level billing reports. She is less concerned with the how and why and more about the “what.” 

**Thinking and Strategy**

Sam takes a systematic approach, focusing on refining workflows, implementing effective technology, and establishing KPIs for predictable revenue and scalable operations. Sam is keen on automating processes wherever possible, and leverages data to monitor provider productivity, claims management, and cash flow.

**Who Sam Is**

She may have a small caseload or no caseload at this point. She is responsible for overseeing the practice's vision and daily administration. Personality traits include:

* Detail-Oriented and Process-Driven: Sam values organization and consistency, preferring solutions that follow standardized protocols.  
* Data-Driven Decision Maker: Sam relies on financial metrics and KPIs to guide decisions and assess practice health.  
* Hands-Off Management Style: Sam prefers to set up systems that empower the team to work independently, minimizing the need for constant oversight while ensuring accountability through clear expectations and metrics.

Systems-Focused Sam may want to outsource, but at her level of growth and profitability, she is looking for a billing optimization partner capable of implementing robust systems and providing in-depth reporting. If she is looking for an outsourced biller, she has likely had 2-3 billers and bad experiences, or she has tried to hire someone in-house, which also didn’t go well. The ideal partner would help Sam reduce AR, establish scalable billing processes, and enhance financial visibility, enabling Sam to focus on strategic growth and operational excellence.

# **Large Group  \- 8-20 doing at least $750k**

## **Persona: Enterprise Erin**

**Overview**

Enterprise Erin leads an extensive practice with 8-20 providers, generating at least $750K in annual revenue. With this scale, Erin’s practice has complex billing and operational requirements. Still, she has figured out the systems needed to onboard clinicians and balance an extensive client base and diverse insurance panels. Erin focuses on optimizing the revenue cycle, managing cash flow, and maintaining efficient processes supporting ongoing growth while attempting to reduce administrative burdens on providers. At this point, Erin has a support staff or two.

**Common Problems and Needs**

* High Volume and Complexity in Billing: With multiple providers and a wide array of insurance plans, Erin’s practice faces intricate billing challenges that require a systematic approach to avoid errors and streamline claims processing.  
* Minimal Mindset: Still focusing on cost-effectiveness and less experience with outsourcing and vendor management leads Enterprise Erin to want and ask for trial periods or special discounts.   
* Significant Aging Claims and Revenue Bottlenecks: Erin is concerned about the volume of aged accounts receivable, which strains cash flow and can impact payroll.   
* Advanced Technology Needs: The practice’s current software and processes are not fully aligned, causing inefficiencies in billing, scheduling, and claims follow-up that delay reimbursements and add to administrative strain and provider dissatisfaction.  
* Need for Financial Transparency and Reporting: Erin needs high-level financial oversight to track performance metrics across the practice, ensure the practice meets its revenue goals, and identify trends and areas for improvement.

**Thinking and Strategy**

Erin takes an executive-level approach to running the practice to standardize workflows, optimize revenue, and align the practice’s operations with financial and growth goals. Erin is focused on metrics such as AR days, claims turnaround time, provider productivity, and cash flow trends to assess performance and make informed decisions.

**Who Erin Is**

As the CEO and Executive Director, Erin strategically directs the practice's operations, revenue goals, and growth initiatives. Key traits include:

* Leadership and Visionary: Erin has a big-picture mindset, continuously seeking ways to evolve and scale the practice while maintaining quality and efficiency.  
* Strategic and Results-Oriented: Erin relies on detailed financial data and cares about the health and well-being of her providers, not primarily the clients they serve.   
* Delegation and Empowerment: Erin values a team-based approach, preferring systems that empower staff to operate while using reporting to maintain visibility and control.

Enterprise Erin may look for a billing partner if she doesn’t want to deal with turnover and has a good handle on the practice's financial management. She will look for a billing partner with flexible solutions to integrate technology, manage high-volume claims, and provide in-depth financial reporting as needed. This partner should streamline billing functions, reduce AR days, and offer strategic insights that align with Erin’s vision for scalable, sustainable growth. She would benefit from a PHC and, at her size, should have the liquidity for an optimization project worth $20-$45k without breaking the bank. 

# **Mega Group \- 20+ Doing at least $1mm**

## **Persona: Mega Martha**

**Overview**

Enterprise Evelyn oversees a mega group practice with over 20 providers, generating $1 million annually. With this level of scale, Evelyn’s practice is more akin to a healthcare enterprise, demanding robust systems, well-placed technology accelerators, streamlined workflows, and a clear idea of her mission, vision, and values to ensure efficient billing and consistent cash flow. Evelyn’s priorities revolve around optimizing revenue cycle management, minimizing administrative overhead, and maintaining tight financial oversight to support sustainable growth and expansion.

**Common Problems and Needs:**

* High Complexity and Volume in Billing: With a large team and a large cross-section of clients, Evelyn’s practice faces substantial claims volume and billing intricacies across various insurance plans and payers, leading to potential bottlenecks and errors.  
* Aging Accounts and Cash Flow Concerns: The practice deals with a high volume of aged claims, which can stall cash flow and disrupt financial planning. Managing AR effectively across multiple providers and insurance carriers is a critical concern.  
* Need for Advanced, Fully Integrated Technology: The billing tools and systems they use must integrate across billing, scheduling, and practice management; this can create inefficiencies and data gaps, hindering financial transparency and responsiveness.  
* Financial Insight and Robust Reporting: Evelyn needs precise, real-time data on metrics such as AR days, revenue per provider, claims resolution rates, and more to manage financial health and guide strategic decisions.  
* Community Impact: Evelyn is known and trusted within her community at this size. She cares deeply about her reputation and the health of her surrounding community. 

**Thinking and Strategy**

Evelyn operates with an enterprise-level mindset, prioritizing sophisticated billing solutions and scalable infrastructure that support a highly organized, data-informed approach to managing the practice. Evelyn leverages financial metrics and KPIs to assess performance, drive decision-making, and identify new opportunities for revenue growth. Efficiency, accuracy, and proactive problem-solving are Evelyn’s top priorities.

**Who Evelyn Is**

As the CEO and COO, Evelyn is a high-level strategist with a clear vision for practice growth and operational excellence. Key characteristics include:

* Visionary and Analytical: Evelyn is a strategic thinker using financial data to make informed decisions, forecast trends, and set ambitious goals.  
* Efficiency-Driven and Tech-Savvy: Evelyn values technology and efficient systems that automate processes, reduce administrative burdens, and provide centralized control.  
* Empowering Leader: Evelyn prefers to empower her team with data-driven systems, allowing for accountability at all levels without micromanaging day-to-day operations.

Enterprise Evelyn is unlikely to seek an outsourced billing partner. If she does, it is because a broader market disruption has significantly impacted HR processes and procedures, particularly burdening her or her. It does not make wise financial sense to outsource to a billing company to manage her billing. Instead, this practice would benefit from a Practice Health Check to gain laser-focused insight into the state of the billing department and then a revenue optimization project that can operate at the same level of excellence that she has come to expect from her clinical team. 

# **National Organization (Ellie, Better Help) \- 100 clinicians+ Doing at least $10mm**

## **Persona: Nationwide Nora**

**Overview**

Nora leads a prominent national organization like Ellie or BetterHelp, overseeing 100+ clinicians and generating $10 million or more in annual revenue. With operations spanning multiple states and a wide-reaching client base, Nora’s organization requires a sophisticated, streamlined billing and revenue cycle management approach that can scale across a vast and complex operation or a billing partner program that allows providers to choose between various pre-approved billing solutions.  Nora focuses on maintaining high efficiency, ensuring consistent cash flow brand recognition, increasing market share, increasing shareholder equity, and leveraging data to optimize the organization’s financial health and strategic initiatives.

**Common Problems and Needs**

* Massive Billing Volume and Complexity: With hundreds of providers and extensive patient interactions, Nora’s organization faces significant billing demands and must manage intricate claims processes across numerous insurance networks.  
* High AR and Need for Cash Flow Optimization: Even minor reimbursement delays can create substantial cash flow issues with such a large-scale operation. Effective aging management is crucial to ensuring consistent revenue streams.  
* Advanced Technology Integration Needs: Nora’s organization requires a fully integrated tech stack to synchronize billing, client management, scheduling, and data reporting at a national level. Disjointed systems can lead to inefficiencies and data silos that impact performance.  
* High-Level Financial Oversight and Compliance: Nora needs real-time, enterprise-grade reporting to maintain transparency, monitor metrics across multiple locations, and ensure compliance with insurance, state, and federal regulations.

**Thinking and Strategy**

Nora operates with an executive mindset, focusing on big-picture strategies and systemized processes that maintain efficiency nationally. She relies heavily on KPIs and advanced analytics to evaluate operational health, support strategic decision-making, and maximize profitability. Nora values scalable, automated systems that reduce manual input and allow her team to focus on delivering value.

**Who Nora Is**

As CEO of a national organization, Nora is a high-level strategist and visionary, overseeing a complex, data-driven operation. Key characteristics include:

* Enterprise-Level Strategic Thinking: Nora anticipates needs across regions and states, identifying scalable solutions and infrastructure that ensure consistency and performance nationwide.  
* Data-Driven and Metrics-Focused: Nora prioritizes comprehensive data reporting and KPIs, using metrics to assess performance, address challenges, and optimize financial and operational processes.  
* Delegative and Results-Oriented Leader: While Nora is hands-off with daily operations, she insists on accountability through structured metrics and expects all departments to work with clear, transparent, and consistent reporting.

Nationwide Nora seeks a billing partner(s) who offers an enterprise-level, tech-driven approach to managing vast billing volumes, providing comprehensive reporting, and aligning with her vision for sustainable growth. The ideal partner should bring deep expertise in handling large-scale operations, robust technology solutions, and insights to streamline the revenue cycle and drive consistent cash flow across her expansive network of providers. Nationwide Nora is obsessed with creating the best brand through provider education and improved organizational excellence. Nationwide Nora has access to legions of practices that could benefit from education, billing, or billing optimization projects.
