# 🧾 Helping Your Patients Understand Insurance

This guide illustrates how financial responsibility is divided between the **patient** and the **insurance company** across three stages of insurance billing. It is designed to make conversations around cost and coverage clearer for both staff and clients.

---

## 🏁 Stage 1: Paying the Deductible  
**Gate 1: Meeting the Deductible**

- **Patient Pays:** 100%  
  _Unless the deductible does not apply for mental health benefits_  
- **Insurance Pays:** 0%  
  _Unless the deductible is waived for mental health_

💡 At this stage, the **patient is fully responsible** for the cost of services until their deductible is met.

---

## 🤝 Stage 2: Cost Sharing with Copay or Coinsurance

After the deductible is met, the patient enters a cost-sharing stage. There are two primary models:

### 💵 Copay  
- **Patient Pays:** A fixed amount (e.g., **$25**) per session  
- **Insurance Pays:** The remainder of the contracted rate

### 📊 Coinsurance  
- **Patient Pays:** A percentage of the contracted rate (e.g., **30%**)  
- **Insurance Pays:** The remaining percentage

💡 This is the most common stage where billing gets confusing—patients may think they're fully covered but are still responsible for part of the cost.

---

## 🛡️ Stage 3: 100% Insurance Coverage  
**Gate 2: Meeting the Out-of-Pocket Max**

Once the patient meets their **out-of-pocket maximum** for the year:

- **Patient Pays:** 0%  
- **Insurance Pays:** 100%

💡 At this point, the **insurance company covers the full cost** of services for the remainder of the year (subject to plan limits and network status).
