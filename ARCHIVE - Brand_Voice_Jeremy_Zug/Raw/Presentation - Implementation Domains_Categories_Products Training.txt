Product Types
Trainings are slide decks that give the customer an overview of knowledge necessary to be successful in a category, with some information that can be customized to their EHR or systems.
Process Creations are templates that are designed to hold step-by-step processes fully customized to the practice and their systems. The end product is a process that anyone within their organization can follow to complete the target task(s) of the process creation.
Documents are templates for tools that support the target activities of process creations, and should be referenced in development of the process creations.
Consultations include providing advice developed for the practice or providing your time to achieve specific tasks in support of the category, such as facilitating interviews, reviewing insurance contracts, or consulting on software.

Implementation System
