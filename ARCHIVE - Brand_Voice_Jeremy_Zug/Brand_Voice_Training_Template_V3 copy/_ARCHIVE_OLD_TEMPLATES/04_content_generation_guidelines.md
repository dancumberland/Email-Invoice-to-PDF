# Content Generation Guidelines
Version 1.0

## Content Creation Framework

### Pre-Generation Analysis
1. Purpose Identification
   - Content objective
   - Target audience
   - Platform requirements
   - Desired outcomes
   - Success metrics

2. Resource Selection
   - Relevant brand stories
   - Appropriate metaphors
   - Visual language elements
   - Brand-specific terminology
   - Historical references

3. Pattern Preparation
   - Voice characteristics
   - Tone requirements
   - Structure needs
   - Technical specifications

### Generation Process

1. Content Structure
   - Opening patterns
   - Development flow
   - Transition techniques
   - Closing approaches
   - Call-to-action styles

2. Element Integration
   - Story placement
   - Metaphor development
   - Visual description
   - Terminology usage
   - Brand reference

3. Quality Control
   - Pattern verification
   - Brand alignment
   - Technical accuracy
   - Audience appropriateness

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Social Media Content

1. LinkedIn Posts
   ```
   Structure:
   - Hook (personal story/observation)
   - Bridge (connection to audience)
   - Lesson (brand-aligned insight)
   - Action (engagement invitation)
   
   Example:
   "Standing in my garden this morning, I noticed how the strongest plants weren't necessarily the biggest...
   
   This reminded me of a conversation with a client last week about sustainable growth...
   
   What I've learned in [X] years of [brand work] is that...
   
   What's your experience with sustainable growth? Share your story below."
   ```

2. Email Newsletters
   ```
   Structure:
   - Personal opening
   - Story integration
   - Value delivery
   - Community connection
   - Clear next step
   
   Example:
   "The morning frost painted my windows with delicate patterns today...
   
   It reminded me of a pattern I've noticed in our [brand] community...
   
   This brings me to a powerful lesson about [brand-specific topic]...
   
   I'd love to hear your thoughts on this. Simply reply to this email and share your perspective."
   ```

3. Blog Posts
   ```
   Structure:
   - Engaging hook
   - Personal connection
   - Value development
   - Story integration
   - Practical application
   - Community invitation
   
   Example:
   "The ancient practice of [topic] holds a secret that transformed how I approach [brand-specific area]...
   
   Let me share a story from our [brand] community that illustrates this..."
   ```

## Content Type Specifications

### Educational Content

1. Teaching Framework
   ```
   Pattern:
   - Context setting
   - Concept introduction
   - Story illustration
   - Practical application
   - Integration guidance
   
   Example:
   "Before we dive into [concept], let's explore why this matters for your [brand-specific journey]...
   
   This reminds me of [client name]'s experience when they first started..."
   ```

2. Case Studies
   ```
   Structure:
   - Situation setup
   - Challenge identification
   - Solution development
   - Implementation process
   - Results and learning
   
   Example:
   "When [client] first joined our community, they were facing a familiar challenge...
   
   Through our work together, they discovered that just like a river finding its path..."
   ```

3. How-To Content
   ```
   Framework:
   - Purpose clarity
   - Process overview
   - Step-by-step guidance
   - Common challenges
   - Success tips
   
   Example:
   "Creating your [brand-specific outcome] is like tending a garden...
   
   Let's walk through each step of this process, starting with..."
   ```

## Brand Voice Verification

### Quality Control Checklist

1. Voice Elements
   - Tone consistency
   - Personal warmth
   - Professional authority
   - Brand authenticity
   - Community connection

2. Content Components
   - Story integration
   - Metaphor usage
   - Visual language
   - Terminology accuracy
   - Value delivery

3. Technical Aspects
   - Structure alignment
   - Format compliance
   - Platform optimization
   - Engagement elements
   - Call-to-action clarity

### Pattern Validation

1. Story Integration Check
   ```
   Weak: "This technique is effective."
   
   Strong: "Last spring, one of our community members discovered this technique when they were struggling with [challenge]. Like a seed breaking through soil..."
   ```

2. Metaphor Usage Check
   ```
   Weak: "Growth takes time and effort."
   
   Strong: "Just as a garden doesn't bloom overnight, your journey in [brand area] unfolds through seasons of growth, each requiring its own kind of nurturing..."
   ```

3. Visual Language Check
   ```
   Weak: "The process has multiple steps."
   
   Strong: "Picture yourself standing at the base of a mountain, each switchback in the trail representing a different phase of your [brand] journey..."
   ```

## Implementation Examples

### Full Content Example

```
Title: Embracing the Seasons of Growth

[Opening - Personal Story + Visual]
This morning, as I watched the first snow of the season dust my garden, I was struck by how the seemingly dormant plants were actually preparing for spring's renewal. It reminded me of a conversation I had with [Client Name] during their early days in our community.

[Development - Story + Teaching]
Like many in our [Brand] family, [Client] was in what felt like a winter season of their [journey/business/growth]. They had the seeds of their dreams planted but couldn't see any visible progress. Through our work together, they learned that just as winter serves a crucial purpose in nature's cycle, these quieter periods in our [brand-specific journey] are actually laying the groundwork for extraordinary growth.

[Value Delivery - Metaphor + Insight]
Think of your [brand-specific area] like a garden through the seasons:
- Spring: Time for planting new ideas and initiatives
- Summer: Period of visible growth and expansion
- Autumn: Season of harvest and reflection
- Winter: Phase of rest and internal strengthening

[Community Connection]
Where do you find yourself in these seasons? What's currently germinating beneath the surface of your [brand-specific journey]?

[Call to Action]
If you're ready to explore how to work with your current season rather than against it, I invite you to [brand-specific next step]. Like a skilled gardener, I'll help you understand the unique needs of your growth phase and how to nurture it appropriately.

Analysis:
- Opens with brand-specific nature metaphor
- Integrates client story
- Uses visual imagery throughout
- Maintains warm, nurturing tone
- Includes teaching element
- Encourages community engagement
- Closes with clear, aligned call to action
```
