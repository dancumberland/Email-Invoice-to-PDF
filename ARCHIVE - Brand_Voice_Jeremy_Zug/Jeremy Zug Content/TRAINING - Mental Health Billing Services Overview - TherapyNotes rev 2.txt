# Mental Health Billing Services Overview  
**Operating in TherapyNotes**

---

## 🛠 TherapyNotes Billing Services

Whether you are already using **TherapyNotes** or not, Practice Solutions can support your billing operations.

### If you *don’t* have a TherapyNotes account:
- They will **create an account** for you based on your practice’s needs.
- Assist in **migrating data** from your existing EHR (you are responsible for the export).
- Ensure everything is set up **in compliance with HIPAA** — their access is *non-clinical*, so your clinical notes remain private.

### If you *already* use TherapyNotes:
- You simply **add a user** for the Practice Solutions setup specialist.
- They’ll review your **billing settings**, configure claim submission (EDI), and remittance (ERA) connections.
- Additional users may be added as needed — all will be non-clinical roles.

📝 **Note:** If you want Practice Solutions to follow up on old claims from your previous EHR, additional charges may apply.

---

## 🤝 Your Dedicated Biller

Once setup is complete, you’ll be assigned a **personal, US-based biller** who manages your day-to-day billing. Here's what the workflow looks like:

**Your role:**
- Add clients in TherapyNotes
- Schedule sessions
- Assign CPT codes
- Enter treatment plans and diagnosis codes

**Their role:**  
On your assigned **bi-weekly billing day**, your biller will handle all billing processes described below.

---

## 🔧 Services Provided

### ✅ Claim Submission
- All claims (in-network, out-of-network, and select EAP) are submitted **electronically** for speed and accuracy.

### ✅ Claim Follow-Up
- Your biller will **track** and follow up on:
  - Denied claims
  - Rejected claims
  - Claims that haven’t been paid in a timely manner
- They also **monitor** insurance/patient payment timelines.

### ✅ Eligibility and Benefits (E&B) Checks
- Requested by you and completed **within 1–2 business days**.
- These checks are essentially quotes.
- ⚠️ **Important:** Insurance companies provide incorrect information roughly **30% of the time**, so checks are not 100% reliable.

### ✅ Payment Posting
- All insurance and patient payments are recorded **directly in TherapyNotes**.
- This keeps **your financial data centralized** and visible in one system.

### ✅ General Billing Support
- You’ll receive a **report every billing day** summarizing all billing activity.
- Your biller is available **during business hours** to answer questions.

## 💰 Pricing Overview

Practice Solutions offers two billing options based on your desired level of support:

### 📦 Base Billing Package
- **One-Time Setup Fee:** Starting at **$100** (based on effort required)
- **Monthly Invoice:**  
  - **6% of gross practice revenue**  
    _Includes: insurance reimbursements, copays, deductibles, and private-pay_
  - **Additional Charges:** Postage (if applicable)

### 📦 Billing with Patient Support
- **One-Time Setup Fee:** Starting at **$100**
- **Monthly Invoice:**  
  - **6% of collected insurance revenue**  
    _Includes: insurance reimbursements, copays, and deductibles only_
  - **Additional Charges:** Postage, plus any requested legacy data migration

🧾 **Tip:** The "Base Billing Package" is better if you want comprehensive support for all payments, including private pay. "Billing with Patient Support" is more narrowly focused on insurance revenue.
