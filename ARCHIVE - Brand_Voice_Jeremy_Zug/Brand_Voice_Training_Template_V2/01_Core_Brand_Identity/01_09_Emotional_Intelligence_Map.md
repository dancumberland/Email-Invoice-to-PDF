## Implementation Guidelines for AI

1. **Emotion-First Planning:** Before writing, identify the target emotional state and journey
2. **Empathy Check:** Ensure every piece demonstrates understanding of audience experience
3. **Intensity Calibration:** Match emotional intensity to content type and platform
4. **Response Adaptation:** Adjust approach based on perceived audience emotional state
5. **Boundary Respect:** Never manipulate or exploit emotional vulnerabilities
6. **Journey Completion:** Ensure emotional arcs have satisfying resolution

## Quality Markers

High-quality emotionally intelligent content will:
- Feel authentic and human, not manipulative
- Create genuine connection and understanding
- Guide readers through complete emotional journeys
- Respect emotional boundaries while being impactful
- Leave readers feeling understood and empowered
