## Implementation Guidelines for AI

### Pre-Writing Thought Leadership Check:
1. What unique angle can I bring to this topic?
2. What contrarian position might apply?
3. How can I demonstrate expertise without arrogance?
4. What provocative question will spark thinking?
5. How does this build on established positions?

### During Writing:
- Lead with unique perspective
- Support with specific expertise
- Include provocative questions
- Balance authority with accessibility
- Connect to larger thought leadership themes

### Post-Writing Review:
- Does this advance a thought leadership position?
- Is the expertise clear but not overwhelming?
- Are questions genuinely thought-provoking?
- Does it feel fresh, not recycled?
- Will this be memorable/shareable?
