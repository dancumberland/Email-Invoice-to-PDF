# Core Brand Identity: Client Offer Ladder

**Objective:** This document guides an AI in analyzing a client's provided materials (e.g., existing product/service documentation, sales materials, business strategy documents, website content) to map out their current or planned products, services, and offers. The AI's role is to synthesize information from these diverse client sources, not to invent offers or details. This map should be structured as an offer ladder, categorizing offerings from lead generation to premium services.

## Offer Ladder Overview
*AI Prompt: Review all provided client materials. First, ascertain if the client has an explicitly defined offer ladder, value ladder, or documented product/service ecosystem structure. If so, summarize that structure here. If not, the AI should prepare to structure the identified offers (from subsequent sections) into a logical ladder format. In either case, note any strategic recommendations or observations made *by the client in their materials* regarding their offer structure (e.g., stated needs to streamline, create clearer pathways, develop new offers for specific segments).*

## Strategic Considerations for Client's Offer Ladder
*AI Prompt: Based on a comprehensive review of all client materials (including any strategic plans, marketing audits, business goals documents, sales performance reports, or customer feedback summaries), synthesize and summarize key strategic insights, challenges, or opportunities *explicitly mentioned or strongly implied by the client* regarding their offer ladder. Focus on extracting the client's own perspectives or documented findings. This might include:
    - Client-identified gaps in the current offer ladder.
    - Documented opportunities for new offers to serve specific ICP segments.
    - Client discussions or plans for improving the flow between offers.
    - Stated pricing strategy considerations or challenges.
    - Explicit connections (or misalignments) noted by the client between their offers and overall business goals.*

*The AI should present these as a summary of the client's own strategic thinking, rather than generating net-new strategic advice beyond direct inferences from the provided documents.*

*This document, once populated by the AI using the client's specific materials, will outline the client's offer ladder. It should be reviewed with the client and updated regularly to reflect the current state of their offerings and strategic focus.*
