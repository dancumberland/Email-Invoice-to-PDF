# 04_01 Platform Guideline AI Generation Guide

## Purpose
This document guides an AI in generating comprehensive, platform-specific brand voice and content guidelines. The AI will analyze core brand identity (Module 01), ICPs (Module 02), general content guidelines (Module 03), and any client-provided examples for a specific platform to create a detailed markdown document for that platform (e.g., `04_02_LinkedIn_Guidelines.md`, `04_03_Instagram_Guidelines.md`, etc.).

## Instructions for AI

When tasked with generating a guideline document for a specific platform (e.g., LinkedIn, Instagram, Blog, YouTube, Podcast):

1.  **Identify Target Platform:** Clearly establish the platform for which the guidelines are being created.
2.  **Deep Analysis of Core Materials:**
    *   Thoroughly review all documents in `01_Core_Brand_Identity/`.
    *   Thoroughly review all documents in `02_ICPs/`, paying attention to which ICPs are most relevant to the target platform.
    *   Thoroughly review all documents in `03_General_Content_Guidelines/`.
    *   Analyze any client-provided examples of successful (or unsuccessful) content for the *target platform*.
3.  **Structure of Generated Platform Guideline Document:**
    The generated markdown file for the platform (e.g., `04_02_LinkedIn_Guidelines.md`) MUST include the following sections, tailored specifically to the target platform based on your analysis:

    *   **`## Overview & Platform Purpose`**
        *   Briefly describe the brand's primary goals for this platform.
        *   How this platform fits into the overall content strategy.

    *   **`## Core [Platform Name] Voice Elements`**
        *   **`### Voice Characteristics on [Platform Name]`**: How the core brand voice (from Module 03) specifically manifests here. Are certain aspects amplified or downplayed?
        *   **`### Tone Balance on [Platform Name]`**: Specific tonal nuances for this platform (e.g., more informal, highly professional, fast-paced, reflective).
        *   **`### Signature Phrases & Language Patterns for [Platform Name]`**: Platform-specific recurring phrases, calls-to-action, or linguistic styles.

    *   **`## Post/Content Structure & Formatting on [Platform Name]`**
        *   **`### Hook Patterns for [Platform Name]`**: Examples of effective opening lines/hooks suitable for the platform.
        *   **`### Visual Structure & Formatting`**: Guidelines on line spacing, paragraph length, use of emojis, hashtags, media (images, video), links, etc.
        *   **`### Content Length & Cadence`**: Optimal length for posts/content and recommended posting frequency.

    *   **`## Key Content Types & Templates for [Platform Name]`** (Inspired by Mel Varghese's LinkedIn guide)
        *   Identify 3-5 primary content types effective on this platform (e.g., Personal Story, Tutorial, Behind-the-Scenes, Q&A, Value Tip, Community Question, Case Study Snippet).
        *   **Important Note on Example Volume:** When generating the examples below, aim for a cumulative total of approximately **1000-2000 words of example content** (this includes both good and poor examples, across all identified content types) for this specific platform. This ensures a rich and practical repository for users to learn from. The examples should be sufficiently detailed to clearly illustrate the application of the brand voice and platform-specific strategies.
        *   For each content type, provide:
            *   **`Template Structure:`** A clear outline or skeleton.
            *   **`Key Elements to Include:`**
            *   **`Good Example(s):`** (Can be hypothetical if no client examples exist, but clearly derived from brand principles).
            *   **`Poor Example(s) / What to Avoid:`**

    *   **`## Engagement Strategy on [Platform Name]`**
        *   **`### Encouraging Interaction`**: Specific tactics for this platform (e.g., types of questions to ask, poll usage, contest ideas).
        *   **`### Responding to Comments/Messages`**: Tone and timeliness for responses.
        *   **`### Community Building Tactics`**: How to foster community on this platform.

    *   **`## Platform-Specific Training Elements`** (Inspired by Mel Varghese's LinkedIn guide)
        *   **`### Success Metrics for [Platform Name]`**: How will success be measured here?
        *   **`### Common Pitfalls on [Platform Name]`**: Mistakes to avoid specific to this platform.
        *   **`### Practical Exercises for [Platform Name]`**: Short exercises for content creators to practice the voice.
        *   **`### QA Checklist for [Platform Name] Content`**: A quick checklist before publishing.

    *   **`## Platform-Specific Best Practices Repository`**
        *   A list of curated best practices for this platform, drawing from general best practices and brand-specific insights.

    *   **`## [Platform Name] Voice Evolution Log`**
        *   A simple log structure (Date, Change/Observation, Reason, Impact) for the client to note how their strategy or voice on this platform evolves over time.

4.  **Output Format:** The generated guideline MUST be a single, well-formatted markdown file named appropriately (e.g., `04_02_LinkedIn_Guidelines.md`, `04_03_Blog_Guidelines.md`).
5.  **Emphasis on Derivation:** Clearly state that all guidelines are derived from the core brand documents (Modules 01-03) and any provided platform examples. Avoid inventing new brand principles.

## Guiding Principles for AI Generation:
*   **Be Specific:** Generic advice is not helpful. Tailor every section to the nuances of the target platform.
*   **Be Actionable:** Provide clear, usable templates, examples, and checklists.
*   **Maintain Consistency:** Ensure the platform-specific voice aligns with the core brand voice, adapting it appropriately rather than creating something entirely new.
*   **Leverage Exemplars:** If the client provided strong examples for the platform, dissect them to extract patterns and best practices. If inspired by external best practices (like Mel Varghese's structure for LinkedIn), adapt them to the client's specific brand voice.
