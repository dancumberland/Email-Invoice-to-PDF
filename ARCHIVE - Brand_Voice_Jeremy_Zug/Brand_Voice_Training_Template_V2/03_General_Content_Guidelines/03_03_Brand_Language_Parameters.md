# General Content Guide: AI Guide: Defining Brand Language Parameters

**Objective for AI:** Your primary task is to analyze client-provided materials (e.g., existing style guides, glossaries, `01_Brand_Foundation.md`, `Linguistic_Patterns.md`, `Content_Generation_Principles.md`, sample content) to establish precise parameters for brand language. These parameters will ensure consistency, clarity, and adherence to the brand's specific communication style across all content.

**Guiding Principles for AI:**
*   **Precision & Clarity:** Parameters must be specific and unambiguous to avoid misinterpretation.
*   **Brand Alignment:** All language parameters should reinforce the brand's identity, values, and voice as defined in foundational documents.
*   **Practicality:** The parameters should be easily applicable by content creators (human or AI).
*   **Comprehensiveness:** Aim to cover key aspects of language use that contribute to a distinct and consistent brand voice.
*   **Evolution:** Recognize that language parameters may need to evolve; provide a framework that can be updated.
*   **Source Prioritization:** If conflicting information arises from different client materials, prioritize the most recent or authoritative sources, or flag for client clarification.

## Brand Language Parameter Definition Process:

*AI Task: For each section below, meticulously review all provided client materials. Synthesize your findings into specific parameters and guidelines. For each parameter, provide an AI prompt for its application or further definition by the client-facing AI.*

### 1. Terminology: Approved & Discouraged

*   **Parameter Definition (AI to generate based on analysis):**
    *   **Approved Terms List:** *e.g., "Always use 'Client Partner' instead of 'Account Manager'. Prioritize 'bespoke solutions' over 'customized services'." (AI to compile a list from materials or identify patterns).*
    *   **Discouraged/Forbidden Terms List:** *e.g., "Avoid industry jargon like 'synergistic leverage' unless specifically defined and necessary for a technical audience. Never use 'cheap' to describe offerings; use 'cost-effective' or 'value-driven'." (AI to compile a list).*
*   **Explanation (AI to generate):** *e.g., "Using consistent, approved terminology reinforces brand messaging and professionalism. Avoiding discouraged terms prevents miscommunication or negative brand perception."*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Review the following list of [Approved Terms]. How will you ensure these are preferentially used in relevant contexts? Provide 3 example sentences using different approved terms.*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Review the following list of [Discouraged/Forbidden Terms]. What mechanism will you use to avoid these terms or flag them for review if their use seems unavoidable in a specific context?*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Are there specific terms related to [Client's Industry/Products/Services] that have nuanced meanings or common misuses? If so, define their correct usage for the brand.*

### 2. Brand-Specific Jargon & Neologisms

*   **Parameter Definition (AI to generate):**
    *   **List of Brand Jargon/Neologisms:** *e.g., "The 'InnovateSphere' refers to our proprietary development framework. 'Client Success Journey' outlines our onboarding and support process." (AI to identify unique terms used by the client).*
    *   **Definition & Context for Each:** *For each term, provide a clear definition and the appropriate context for its use.*
    *   **Introduction Guidelines:** *e.g., "When first introducing a brand-specific term to a new audience, provide a brief explanation or ensure context makes its meaning clear."*
*   **Explanation (AI to generate):** *e.g., "Using brand-specific terms consistently can build a unique identity and a sense of community, but they must be introduced and used clearly to avoid confusing the audience."*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Here is a list of brand-specific terms: [List]. For each, confirm its definition and provide an example sentence demonstrating its correct usage in a typical communication scenario.*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: When introducing one of these brand-specific terms to an audience potentially unfamiliar with it, what is the preferred method: a direct definition, contextual explanation, or a link to a glossary (if available)?*

### 3. Inclusivity & Accessibility in Language

*   **Parameter Definition (AI to generate, referencing `Linguistic_Patterns.md` and general best practices):**
    *   **People-First Language:** *e.g., "Always use people-first language (e.g., 'people with disabilities' not 'disabled people')."*
    *   **Gender-Neutral Language:** *e.g., "Employ gender-neutral terms (e.g., 'team,' 'colleagues,' 'they' as singular when gender is unknown or irrelevant) instead of gendered terms like 'guys' or 'manpower'."*
    *   **Avoidance of Stereotypes & Bias:** *e.g., "Ensure language avoids reinforcing cultural, racial, age-related, or gender stereotypes."*
    *   **Accessibility Considerations:** *e.g., "Write clear and concise sentences. Use descriptive alt text for images. Avoid using color alone to convey meaning. Ensure link text is descriptive." (This may overlap with web/content format guidelines but is crucial for language itself).*
*   **Explanation (AI to generate):** *e.g., "Inclusive and accessible language ensures content is respectful, welcoming, and understandable to the widest possible audience, reflecting positive brand values."*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Review the guidelines for [Inclusivity and Accessibility]. How will you proactively check generated content for adherence to these principles? Describe any specific checks or tools you might use (e.g., checking for gendered pronouns, ableist language).*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Provide 2-3 examples of rephrasing potentially non-inclusive statements into inclusive ones, relevant to the client's typical communication topics.*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: What is the brand's stance on using emojis for inclusivity or emotional expression? Are there preferred/discouraged emojis? (Refer to `Linguistic_Patterns.md` if applicable).*

### 4. Formality, Humor, and Technicality Levels

*   **Parameter Definition (AI to generate, referencing `01_Brand_Foundation.md` and `Linguistic_Patterns.md`):**
    *   **Overall Formality:** *e.g., "Maintain a professional yet approachable tone. Avoid overly casual slang but also overly stiff, academic language. Contractions (e.g., 'it's,' 'we're') are generally acceptable."*
    *   **Use of Humor:** *e.g., "Humor should be witty and intelligent, never sarcastic, offensive, or at others' expense. Use sparingly and only when contextually appropriate for [Target ICPs]." (Specify if humor is generally encouraged or discouraged).*
    *   **Technicality Level:** *e.g., "For general audiences (e.g., blog, social media), explain technical concepts in simple terms. For expert audiences (e.g., white papers, specific ICP segments), appropriate technical language can be used, but define acronyms on first use."*
*   **Explanation (AI to generate):** *e.g., "Defining these parameters ensures the brand communicates appropriately for its identity and audience, whether aiming to be authoritative, relatable, or engaging."*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Describe the typical level of formality for [Content Type, e.g., LinkedIn posts vs. technical documentation]. Provide an example of a sentence written too formally and how you would adjust it, and one written too informally and how you would adjust it.*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: If humor is permissible, provide an example of brand-appropriate humor and an example of humor that would be off-brand. What topics are strictly off-limits for humor?*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: When communicating a complex technical detail from [Client's Product/Service] to a non-technical ICP, what is your strategy for simplification without losing accuracy? Provide an example.*

### 5. Numbers, Dates, Acronyms, Abbreviations & Capitalization

*   **Parameter Definition (AI to generate based on common style guides or client preferences if available):**
    *   **Numbers:** *e.g., "Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above. For large numbers, use numerals (e.g., 1,000). Use commas in numbers with four or more digits (e.g., 1,234, not 1234)."*
    *   **Dates:** *e.g., "Use the format Month Day, Year (e.g., January 1, 2024). Avoid ordinal numbers (e.g., January 1st)."*
    *   **Acronyms & Abbreviations:** *e.g., "Spell out acronyms on first use, followed by the acronym in parentheses, e.g., Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). Thereafter, ICP can be used. Avoid excessive use of abbreviations."*
    *   **Capitalization (Headlines/Titles):** *e.g., "Use title case for headlines and subheadings (e.g., Capitalize Principal Words). For blog post titles, sentence case might be preferred: [Client to specify or AI to suggest based on existing content]."*
    *   **Capitalization (Brand Names/Products):** *e.g., "Always capitalize 'BrandName' and 'ProductNameX' as specified."*
*   **Explanation (AI to generate):** *e.g., "Consistency in these stylistic details contributes to professionalism and readability."*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Review the guidelines for [Numbers, Dates, Acronyms, Capitalization]. How will you ensure these are applied consistently? Will you refer to a specific style guide (e.g., AP, Chicago) if the client doesn't have explicit preferences for unlisted items?*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Provide an example of a sentence that incorrectly uses 2-3 of these parameters, and then show the corrected version.*
*   **AI Prompt for Client-Facing AI:** *AI Prompt: Are there any industry-specific acronyms or abbreviations that are so common they do not need to be spelled out on first use for [Target ICPs]? List them if so.*
