## 3. Story Adaptation Guidelines

### Platform-Specific Adaptations

#### LinkedIn Version:
*   **Length:** 150-300 words
*   **Focus:** Professional insight/lesson
*   **Opening:** Hook within first line
*   **Structure:** Story → Insight → Question/CTA

#### Blog Version:
*   **Length:** 500-1500 words
*   **Focus:** Deeper exploration

*   **Opening:** Conversational entry

## Advanced Techniques

### The "Story Stack"
Layer multiple micro-stories for compound effect:
1. Personal anecdote (relatability)
2. Client example (proof)
3. Industry insight (authority)
4. Vision story (inspiration)

### The "Mirror Method"
Start with audience's story, then reveal it's actually about them:
- "A founder once told me..." [describes audience's exact situation]
- Gradual revelation that this could be their story
- Pivot to transformation possibility

### The "Nested Loop"
Start multiple story threads, resolve in reverse order:
- Creates cognitive tension
- Maintains engagement
- Allows complex idea development
- Satisfying when loops close
