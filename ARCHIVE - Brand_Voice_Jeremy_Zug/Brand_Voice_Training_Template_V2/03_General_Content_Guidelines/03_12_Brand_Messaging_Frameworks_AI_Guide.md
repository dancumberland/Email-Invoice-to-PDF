# 03_12 Brand Messaging Frameworks AI Guide

## Purpose
This document guides an AI in assisting the client to develop and apply key brand messaging frameworks. By processing information from Modules 01 (Core Brand Identity) and 02 (ICPs), the AI can help articulate the brand's message in various structured ways, leading to more effective and consistent communication across all platforms.

## General Instructions for AI
For each framework outlined below:
1.  Thoroughly analyze all relevant documents in `01_Core_Brand_Identity/` (especially `01_01_Brand_Story_And_Narrative_Elements.md`, `01_02_Offer_Ladder_And_Unique_Value_Proposition.md`, `01_05_Target_Audience_Overview_And_Pain_Points.md`) and `02_ICPs/`.
2.  Prompt the client to specify which product, service, or aspect of their brand they wish to apply the framework to, if not already clear.
3.  Generate a completed, structured version of the framework based on the client's information and your analysis.
4.  Provide a brief explanation of how the client can leverage the completed framework to generate various types of content (e.g., website copy, email sequences, social media posts, ad copy, video scripts).
5.  Ensure all language used in the generated frameworks aligns with the brand's voice as defined in `03_01_Brand_Voice_And_Tone.md` and other relevant Module 03 documents.

## 1. Jobs To Be Done (JTBD)

### Overview
The Jobs To Be Done framework focuses on understanding the underlying "job" a customer is trying to get done when they "hire" a product or service. It looks beyond features to the customer's real motivation and desired outcome, encompassing functional, social, and emotional aspects.

### Key Components
*   **The "Job":** The fundamental task or goal the customer is trying to accomplish.
*   **The "Situation":** The context or circumstances in which the job arises.
*   **The "Motivation":** The driving force behind needing to get the job done.
*   **The "Desired Outcome":** What success looks like for the customer once the job is done effectively.
*   **Obstacles/Struggles:** Pain points or difficulties faced in current solutions or without a solution.
*   **Hiring Criteria:** Factors influencing the choice of one solution over another.

*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "Let's explore the 'Job To Be Done' for your customers. Please describe your primary offer and the target ICP for this exercise. What are their biggest struggles in getting this done now, and what does their ideal outcome look like?"

## 2. StoryBrand (BrandScript by Donald Miller)

### Overview
StoryBrand utilizes a 7-part narrative framework (SB7) to clarify a brand's message, positioning the customer as the hero and the brand as the empathetic and authoritative guide.

### Key Components (The SB7 Framework)
1.  **A Character (The Customer/Hero):** Who are they? What do they want (as it relates to the brand)?
2.  **Has a Problem (The Villain, External, Internal, Philosophical):** What obstacles do they face?
3.  **And Meets a Guide (The Brand):** How does the brand show empathy and authority?
4.  **Who Gives Them a Plan (The Process):** What clear, simple steps can the customer take?
5.  **And Calls Them to Action (Direct & Transitional CTAs):** What should they do next?
6.  **That Helps Them Avoid Failure (The Stakes):** What are the negative consequences of not acting?
7.  **And Ends in Success (The Transformation):** What does their improved life/situation look like?

### AI Prompts & Output Structure
*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "We'll use the StoryBrand framework to clarify your message. For your [specified product/service or overall brand], who is the customer (hero)? What key problem do they face (external, internal, philosophical)? How can your brand act as their guide (showing empathy and authority)? What is a simple plan you offer? What are your main calls to action? What failure are you helping them avoid, and what success will they achieve?"

## 3. Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)

### Overview
PAS is a classic copywriting formula designed to capture attention by identifying a pain point, intensifying the emotional impact of that pain, and then presenting the brand's offering as the ideal solution.

### Key Components
*   **Problem:** Clearly and empathetically state a significant pain point the target audience experiences.
*   **Agitate:** Elaborate on the problem. Describe the frustrations, consequences, emotions, and deeper implications associated with it. Make the pain more vivid and relatable.
*   **Solution:** Introduce the brand's product/service as the clear, effective, and timely solution to this agitated problem.

### AI Prompts & Output Structure
*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "Let's craft some PAS messages. For your [specified product/service] targeting [specified ICP], what is the core problem they face? How can we describe the agitation and frustration this problem causes in more detail? Then, how does your offer solve it?"
*   **AI Generation Task:** "Generate 2-3 distinct PAS framework examples for the client's product/service. Each example should be suitable for a different potential use case (e.g., email subject & opening, social media post, short ad copy snippet)."
*   **Output Structure (for each example):**
    *   **Target Use Case:** (e.g., Email Opening)
    *   **Problem:** [Concise statement of the problem]
    *   **Agitate:** [Elaboration on the problem's impact and emotions]
    *   **Solution:** [Introduction of the product/service as the answer]
    *   **Example Application Snippet:** [e.g., "Headline: Still struggling with X? It's frustrating when Y happens, isn't it? Discover Z and finally achieve [benefit]!"]

## 4. Before-After-Bridge (BAB)

### Overview
The BAB framework highlights the transformation a customer experiences by using a product/service. It paints a picture of their current undesirable state (Before), their desired future state (After), and positions the brand's offering as the Bridge to get them there.

### Key Components
*   **Before:** Describe the customer's current situation, including their problems, frustrations, limitations, and negative emotions.
*   **After:** Paint a vivid picture of their life or situation once their problem is solved using the product/service, highlighting the benefits, desired outcomes, and positive emotions.
*   **Bridge:** Clearly introduce the product/service as the specific means (the bridge) to get from the "Before" state to the "After" state.

### AI Prompts & Output Structure
*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "Describe a typical client transformation using your [specified product/service]. What is their life like 'Before' they use your offer (their pains, challenges)? What does their ideal 'After' state look like (their achievements, feelings)? How does your offer act as the 'Bridge'?"
*   **AI Generation Task:** "Develop a compelling Before-After-Bridge narrative. Focus on tangible and emotional shifts. Provide a summary and a short example story snippet."
*   **Output Structure:**
    *   **The "Before" State:** (Detailed description of current pains, challenges, emotions)
    *   **The "After" State:** (Vivid description of desired outcomes, benefits, new reality, positive emotions)
    *   **The "Bridge" (Your Offer):** (Explanation of how the product/service facilitates this transformation, key features/benefits involved)
    *   **Example Transformation Story Snippet:** (A short, engaging narrative illustrating the BAB)

## 5. Golden Circle (Simon Sinek - Why, How, What)

### Overview
The Golden Circle framework, popularized by Simon Sinek, emphasizes communicating from the inside out: starting with "Why" (the purpose, cause, or belief), then "How" (the specific actions, processes, or unique differentiators), and finally "What" (the products/services offered).

### Key Components
*   **Why:** The core purpose, cause, or belief that drives the brand. Why does the brand exist beyond making money?
*   **How:** The specific actions, proprietary processes, or unique values that differentiate the brand and bring its "Why" to life.
*   **What:** The tangible products or services the brand offers as a result of its "Why" and "How."

### AI Prompts & Output Structure
*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "Let's define your Golden Circle. Drawing from your brand's mission, vision, and core values (Module 01), what is your fundamental 'Why' – your core purpose or belief? How do you uniquely deliver on this 'Why' – what are your key differentiators or processes? And what products/services do you offer ('What') as a result?"
*   **AI Generation Task:** "Based on Module 01 and client input, articulate the brand's Golden Circle. Define its 'Why,' 'How,' and 'What' clearly and concisely."
*   **Output Structure:**
    *   **Why (Our Core Purpose/Belief):** [Clear, compelling statement]
    *   **How (Our Unique Process/Differentiators):** [Explanation of unique methods, values, or actions]
    *   **What (Our Offerings):** [List of products/services that manifest the Why and How]

## 6. Open-Closed-Action (OCA)

### Overview
The Open-Closed-Action framework is a straightforward structure for persuasive communication, useful for presentations, short-form content, or calls to action. It aims to capture attention, deliver a core message, and drive a specific response.

### Key Components
*   **Open:** Start with a compelling hook – a question, surprising statistic, bold statement, or relatable scenario – to grab the audience's attention and introduce the topic or problem.
*   **Closed:** Present the core message, argument, solution, or value proposition. This section should build the case logically and persuasively, leading towards the desired action.
*   **Action:** Clearly and directly state the specific action you want the audience to take next. Make it easy to understand and follow.

### AI Prompts & Output Structure
*   **Prompt for AI (to client):** "Consider a specific message or offer you want to promote. What would be a strong 'Open' to grab attention? What is the core information or value proposition ('Closed') you need to convey? And what specific 'Action' do you want the audience to take?"
*   **AI Generation Task:** "For the client's specified message/offer, generate 1-2 Open-Closed-Action outlines. Each outline should be adaptable for a short piece of content (e.g., social media caption, email CTA section, brief video script idea)."
*   **Output Structure (for each example):**
    *   **Target Scenario/Content Idea:**
    *   **Open (The Hook):** [e.g., "Did you know that X% of [target audience] struggle with Y?"]
    *   **Closed (The Core Message/Value Proposition):** [e.g., "Our [product/service] addresses this by [key benefit/feature], enabling you to [achieve desired outcome]."]
    *   **Action (The Clear Call to Action):** [e.g., "Click the link to learn more and claim your [incentive] today!" or "Reply to this email to book your consultation."]
    *   **Example Application Snippet:** (A short paragraph or script demonstrating the OCA structure)

## AI Application Guidance for Messaging Frameworks
*   **Iterative Process:** Remind the client that developing these frameworks is often an iterative process. The AI-generated drafts are starting points for refinement.
*   **Client Input is Key:** Emphasize that the AI's output will be significantly enhanced by specific and thoughtful client input in response to the initial prompts.
*   **Cross-Referencing:** Always ensure the AI cross-references the generated framework content with `03_01_Brand_Voice_And_Tone.md`, `03_04_Vocabulary_And_Terminology_Guide.md`, and other relevant Module 03 documents to maintain brand voice consistency.
*   **Strategic Selection:** Advise the client to select 1-2 core frameworks that resonate most strongly with their brand and current marketing objectives to focus on initially, rather than attempting to implement all frameworks simultaneously.
*   **Content Generation:** Explain that these completed frameworks are powerful tools for generating a wide array of content, including website copy, landing pages, email marketing sequences, social media campaigns, video scripts, sales presentations, and more. The AI can be further prompted to generate specific content pieces based on these frameworks (e.g., "Using the completed StoryBrand BrandScript, write a homepage hero section.").
